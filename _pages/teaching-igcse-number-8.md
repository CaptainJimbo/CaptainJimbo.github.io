Changing between metric units of **length, area and volume**, where the conversion factor gets squared or cubed, and the three **compound measures** of the course: speed, density and pressure.

<div class="kp" markdown="1">
<h4>Units of length</h4>

- $1$ km $=1000$ m, $1$ m $=100$ cm, $1$ cm $=10$ mm. $5$ mm $=5\div1000\div1000$ km $=5\times10^{-6}$ km.

<h4>Units of area</h4>

- Square the length factor: $1$ m² $=100\times100=10^4$ cm², $1$ km² $=10^3\times10^3=10^6$ m², $1$ cm² $=10^2$ mm². Picture a square 1 km by 1 km made of metre squares.

<h4>Units of volume</h4>

- Cube the length factor: $1$ m³ $=10^6$ cm³, $1$ cm³ $=10^3$ mm³, $1$ m³ $=10^9$ mm³. $1$ litre $=1000$ cm³, $1$ ml $=1$ cm³.

<h4>Compound measures</h4>

- **Speed** $=\dfrac{\text{distance}}{\text{time}}$. km/h → m/s: $\times1000\div3600$ (i.e. $\div3.6$); m/s → km/h: $\times3.6$. $120$ km/h $=33\tfrac{1}{3}$ m/s.
- **Density** $=\dfrac{\text{mass}}{\text{volume}}$, usually g/cm³ or kg/m³. $100$ cm³ of lead with mass $1134$ g: $11.34$ g/cm³. To convert kg/m³ to g/cm³ divide by 1000.
- **Pressure** $=\dfrac{\text{force}}{\text{area}}$, in N/m² (or N/cm²). Rearrange: force $=$ pressure $\times$ area.
- Convert to **matching units** before dividing (grams with cm³, newtons with m²), and make the time unit match what the answer needs (per second, per minute…).
</div>

<div class="ex" markdown="1">
<b class="lbl">Worked example.</b> A cheetah runs at 30 m/s. What is this in km/h?

In one hour it covers $30\times3600=108\,000$ m $=108$ km, so $108$ km/h.
</div>

<div class="ex" markdown="1">
<b class="lbl">Worked example.</b> A block of metal 20 cm × 5 cm × 4 cm has mass 3.2 kg. Find its density in g/cm³.

Volume $=400$ cm³, mass $=3200$ g, density $=3200\div400=8$ g/cm³.
</div>

**Common slips:** multiplying an area by 100 instead of $100^2$ · leaving mass in kg with volume in cm³ · dividing the wrong way round (speed = time ÷ distance) · forgetting to convert minutes to seconds.

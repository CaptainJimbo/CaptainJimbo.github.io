<div class="kp" markdown="1">
<h4>5.1 Substitution</h4>

- Replace each letter with its value, then follow the order of operations. Put negative numbers in brackets: with $y=-1$, $5-y=5-(-1)=6$.

<h4>5.2 Inequalities</h4>

- $\lt$ less than · $\le$ less than or equal to · $\gt$ greater than · $\ge$ greater than or equal to.
- On a number line: an **open circle** for $\lt$ or $\gt$ (value not included), a **filled circle** for $\le$ or $\ge$ (value included).
- Solve an inequality like an equation — but if you **multiply or divide by a negative number, reverse the sign**: $-2x\le6$ gives $x\ge-3$.
- A double inequality is solved by doing the same to all three parts: $-7\le2x+1\lt9$ gives $-4\le x\lt4$.

<h4>5.3 Index laws</h4>

- $x^{a}\times x^{b}=x^{a+b}$ · $x^{a}\div x^{b}=x^{a-b}$ · $(x^{a})^{b}=x^{ab}$ · $x^{0}=1$ · $x^{-n}=\dfrac{1}{x^{n}}$
- Deal with the numbers separately from the letters: $3p^{2}\times4p^{-5}=12p^{-3}$.

<h4>5.4–5.5 Equations, identities and formulae</h4>

- An **expression** has no equals sign. An **equation** is true for particular values. An **identity** ($\equiv$) is true for *all* values. A **formula** is a rule linking variables.
- Unknown on both sides: collect the letters on one side. Fractions: multiply both sides by the denominators first.

<h4>5.6 Changing the subject</h4>

- Undo the operations one at a time, doing the same to both sides: $h=4x+7$ gives $x=\dfrac{h-7}{4}$.
- If the new subject appears **twice**, collect those terms on one side and **factorise**.
</div>

<div class="ex" markdown="1">
<b class="lbl">Worked example.</b> Make $x$ the subject of $ax-3=bx+c$.

Collect the $x$ terms on one side: $ax-bx=c+3$. Factorise: $x(a-b)=c+3$. Divide: $x=\dfrac{c+3}{a-b}$.
</div>

**Common slips:** forgetting to reverse the sign after dividing by a negative · $6a^{0}$ written as 1 (only $a^{0}$ is 1, so the answer is 6) · multiplying only one term by the denominator · substituting $-1$ without brackets · listing an end value that a strict inequality excludes.

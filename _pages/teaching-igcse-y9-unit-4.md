A **construction** is an accurate drawing made with a ruler and compasses — no guessing and no measuring angles unless you are told to. Architects, engineers and designers rely on the same handful of techniques.

<div class="kp" markdown="1">
<h4>4.1 Constructing shapes</h4>

- **Three sides (SSS):** draw one side, then draw an arc from each end with the compasses set to the other two lengths. The arcs cross at the third vertex.
- **Two sides and the angle between (SAS)** or **two angles and a side (ASA):** use a ruler and protractor.
- A triangle is only possible if the two shorter sides add to **more than** the longest side.
- An accurate **net** uses the same constructions for each face.

<h4>4.2 Bisectors</h4>

- **Perpendicular bisector of a line:** equal arcs from each end, above and below; join the crossing points. It cuts the line in half at $90^\circ$, and every point on it is the **same distance from both ends**.
- **Angle bisector:** an arc from the vertex across both arms, then equal arcs from those two points; join the vertex to where they cross. Every point on it is the **same distance from both arms**.

<h4>4.3 Perpendiculars and special angles</h4>

- **Perpendicular from a point to a line:** an arc from the point that cuts the line twice, then bisect between the two cuts. Its length is the **shortest distance** from the point to the line.
- **$60^\circ$:** construct an equilateral triangle. **$30^\circ$:** bisect it. **$90^\circ$:** perpendicular bisector. **$45^\circ$:** bisect the right angle.
- Always **leave your construction arcs** — they are the evidence of your method.
</div>

<div class="ex" markdown="1">
<b class="lbl">Worked example.</b> A point must be the same distance from two posts A and B, 8 m apart, and exactly 5 m from each. Describe how to find it on a scale drawing of 1 cm to 1 m, and say how far it is from the line AB.

Draw AB 8 cm long and construct its perpendicular bisector. Set the compasses to 5 cm and draw an arc from A; it crosses the bisector at the point. Distance from AB: $\sqrt{5^{2}-4^{2}}=3$ cm, so 3 m.
</div>

**Common slips:** rubbing out the construction arcs · changing the compass radius between a pair of arcs · using a protractor when the question says compasses only · measuring a slanted distance instead of the perpendicular one.

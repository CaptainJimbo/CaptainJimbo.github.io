Shape and Space 9 — 3D trigonometry

Exercises

Calculator allowed. Marks in brackets. ★ = harder question. Give lengths to 3 s.f. and angles to 1 d.p. unless told otherwise. Diagrams are not drawn accurately.

  1. [6]ABCDEFGH is a cuboid with EH $=8$ cm, HG $=6$ cm and CG $=5$ cm. Find a the length EG  b the angle between the diagonal EC and the base EFGH.

    a Triangle EHG is right-angled at H: $\text{EG}=\sqrt{8^2+6^2}=\sqrt{100}=10$ cm.

    b The angle between EC and the base is angle CEG, in the right-angled triangle CEG (CG is vertical): $\tan\theta=\dfrac{\text{CG}}{\text{EG}}=\dfrac{5}{10}\Rightarrow\theta=26.6^\circ$.

  2. [13]ABCDE is a square-based pyramid. The base BCDE is horizontal with side 12 cm, and AB $=$ AC $=$ AD $=$ AE $=15$ cm. M is the centre of the base, so AM is vertical.
    a Calculate i BD  ii BM  iii AM.b Calculate the angle that AD makes with the base.c Calculate the angle between AM and the face ABC.

    a i BD is the diagonal of the square base: $\sqrt{12^2+12^2}=12\sqrt2=16.97$ cm.  ii $\text{BM}=\tfrac12\text{BD}=8.485$ cm.  iii Triangle ABM is right-angled at M: $\text{AM}=\sqrt{15^2-8.485^2}=\sqrt{153}=12.37$ cm.

    b Angle ADM, with DM $=$ BM: $\cos\theta=\dfrac{\text{DM}}{\text{AD}}=\dfrac{8.485}{15}\Rightarrow\theta=55.6^\circ$.

    c Let N be the mid-point of BC; AN lies in the face ABC and MN $=6$ cm (half the side). The angle between AM and the face is angle MAN in the right-angled triangle AMN: $\tan\phi=\dfrac{\text{MN}}{\text{AM}}=\dfrac{6}{12.37}\Rightarrow\phi=25.9^\circ$.

  3. [6]The diagram shows the roof of a kiosk. ABCD is a horizontal rectangle with AB $=6$ m and BC $=4.5$ m. The point X is vertically above A, and the edge XB makes an angle of 60° with the horizontal. Calculate the angle that the ridge XC makes with the horizontal.

    First the height: in the vertical right-angled triangle XAB, $\text{XA}=\text{AB}\tan60^\circ=6\tan60^\circ=10.39$ m.

    Then the horizontal distance from A to C, the diagonal of the rectangle: $\text{AC}=\sqrt{6^2+4.5^2}=7.5$ m.

    Finally in the vertical triangle XAC: $\tan\theta=\dfrac{\text{XA}}{\text{AC}}=\dfrac{10.39}{7.5}\Rightarrow\theta=54.2^\circ$.

Total: 25 marks

Algebra 1 — Simplifying expressions · Brackets · Solving equations

Exercises

No calculator. Marks are shown in brackets. ★ = harder question (grades 7–9). Check every solution by substituting it back into the equation.

A · Simplify as much as possible

  1. [1]$7pq-2qp$

    $pq$ and $qp$ are the same term (order of multiplication does not matter): $7pq-2pq=5pq$.

  2. [1]$3a^2b-ab^2+2ba^2+4b^2a$

    Collect like terms: $a^2b$ terms: $3a^2b+2a^2b=5a^2b$; $ab^2$ terms: $-ab^2+4ab^2=3ab^2$. Answer $5a^2b+3ab^2$ — the two kinds cannot be combined.

  3. [1]$3c^3\times4c^2$

    Multiply the numbers, add the powers: $12c^5$.

  4. [1]$2y\times(3y)^2$

    $(3y)^2=9y^2$, so $2y\times9y^2=18y^3$.

  5. [2]$7m-(3n-2m)+n$

    The minus applies to both terms in the bracket: $7m-3n+2m+n=9m-2n$.

B · Solve for $x$

  1. [2]$4=\dfrac{x}{9}$

    Multiply both sides by 9: $x=36$. Check: $36\div9=4$ ✓

  2. [2]$4=\dfrac{36}{x}$

    Multiply both sides by $x$: $4x=36$, so $x=9$. Check: $36\div9=4$ ✓

  3. [2]$6(3-2x)=6$

    Either divide both sides by 6 first: $3-2x=1 \Rightarrow 2x=2 \Rightarrow x=1$; or expand: $18-12x=6 \Rightarrow 12x=12 \Rightarrow x=1$. Check: $6(3-2)=6$ ✓

  4. [2]$5x+7=31-3x$

    Add $3x$ to both sides: $8x+7=31$. Subtract 7: $8x=24$, so $x=3$. Check: $15+7=22$ and $31-9=22$ ✓

  5. [2]$3(x-1)-(x+4)=5$

    Expand carefully: $3x-3-x-4=5 \Rightarrow 2x-7=5 \Rightarrow 2x=12 \Rightarrow x=6$. Check: $3\times5-10=5$ ✓

C · Problems leading to equations

  1. [3]The sum of three consecutive even numbers is 150. What are the numbers?

    Let the smallest be $x$; the others are $x+2$ and $x+4$. Then $x+(x+2)+(x+4)=150 \Rightarrow 3x+6=150 \Rightarrow 3x=144 \Rightarrow x=48$. The numbers are 48, 50, 52. Check: $48+50+52=150$ ✓

  2. [3]Three angles meet on a straight line. Their sizes are $(3x+10)^\circ$, $2x^\circ$ and $(4x-10)^\circ$. Find $x$ and the size of each angle.

    Angles on a straight line add up to $180^\circ$: $3x+10+2x+4x-10=180 \Rightarrow 9x=180 \Rightarrow x=20$. The angles are $70^\circ$, $40^\circ$ and $70^\circ$ (sum 180 ✓).

  3. [3]An isosceles triangle has two equal sides of length $(2x+5)$ cm and $(4x-7)$ cm, and a base of $3x$ cm. Find $x$ and the perimeter of the triangle.

    Equal sides: $2x+5=4x-7 \Rightarrow 12=2x \Rightarrow x=6$. Sides: $17$, $17$ and $18$ cm, so the perimeter is $17+17+18=52$ cm.

  4. [3]The length of a rectangle is 3 cm more than its width. Its perimeter is 42 cm. Find the area of the rectangle.

    Width $w$, length $w+3$. Perimeter: $2(w+w+3)=42 \Rightarrow 4w+6=42 \Rightarrow w=9$. So the rectangle is $9\times12$ and the area is $108$ cm².

  5. [3]Ana is 4 years older than Ben. In 5 years' time the sum of their ages will be 40. How old is each of them now?

    Ben is $b$, Ana is $b+4$. In 5 years: $(b+5)+(b+4+5)=40 \Rightarrow 2b+14=40 \Rightarrow b=13$. Ben is 13, Ana is 17. Check: in 5 years $18+22=40$ ✓

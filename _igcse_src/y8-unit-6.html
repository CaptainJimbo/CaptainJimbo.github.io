Pre-IGCSE Mathematics · Year 8 · Unit 6

Fractions and decimals — Rounding · Multiplying and dividing · Recurring decimals · Mixed numbers

Exercises

No calculator. Give fractions in their simplest form. ★ = challenge question.

A · Decimals

  1. Round to 2 decimal places:
    a $47.826$b $3.1049$c $0.096\,51$

    Look at the 3rd decimal place. a $47.83$  b $3.10$ (keep the zero to show 2 d.p.)  c $0.10$.

  2. Round to 3 decimal places:
    a $8.12345$b $0.045\,678$c $219.9996$

    a $8.123$  b $0.046$  c $220.000$ — rounding up carries all the way through.

  3. Use a written method to calculate a $3.4\times2.6$   b $14.5\times12.3$

    a $34\times26=884$; two decimal places in the question → $8.84$.

    b $145\times123=17\,835$; two decimal places → $178.35$.

  4. Calculate a $0.5208\div0.004$   b $71.4\div0.03$

    Make the divisor a whole number by multiplying both numbers by the same power of 10. a $520.8\div4=130.2$.  b $7140\div3=2380$.

  5. Write each recurring decimal using dot notation:
    a $0.5555\ldots$b $0.454545\ldots$c $0.217217\ldots$

    a $0.\dot{5}$  b $0.\dot{4}\dot{5}$  c $0.\dot{2}1\dot{7}$ — dots on the first and last digits of the repeating block.

  6. Write each fraction as a decimal (use dot notation if it recurs):
    a $\dfrac{4}{9}$b $\dfrac{7}{12}$c $\dfrac{3}{8}$d $\dfrac{5}{11}$

    Divide top by bottom. a $0.\dot{4}$  b $0.58\dot{3}$  c $0.375$  d $0.\dot{4}\dot{5}$.

B · Fractions

  1. Calculate
    a $\dfrac{2}{5}\times\dfrac{10}{11}$b $-\dfrac{3}{8}\times\dfrac{4}{9}$c $\dfrac{7}{12}\times\left(-\dfrac{3}{14}\right)$d $-\dfrac{5}{6}\times\left(-\dfrac{9}{10}\right)$

    Cancel first, then multiply; sign rules as for integers. a $\dfrac{4}{11}$  b $-\dfrac{1}{6}$  c $-\dfrac{1}{8}$  d $+\dfrac{3}{4}$.

  2. Evaluate
    a $\dfrac{1}{4}\div\dfrac{1}{3}$b $\dfrac{3}{5}\div\dfrac{1}{10}$c $-\dfrac{2}{3}\div\dfrac{5}{9}$d $-\dfrac{7}{8}\div\left(-\dfrac{7}{12}\right)$

    Multiply by the reciprocal. a $\dfrac{1}{4}\times3=\dfrac{3}{4}$  b $\dfrac{3}{5}\times10=6$  c $-\dfrac{2}{3}\times\dfrac{9}{5}=-\dfrac{6}{5}=-1\tfrac{1}{5}$  d $\dfrac{7}{8}\times\dfrac{12}{7}=\dfrac{3}{2}=1\tfrac{1}{2}$.

  3. Alex ran $1\tfrac{3}{4}$ miles, swam $\dfrac{2}{3}$ of a mile and cycled $4\tfrac{5}{6}$ miles. How far did he travel in total?

    Common denominator 12: $\dfrac{7}{4}+\dfrac{2}{3}+\dfrac{29}{6}=\dfrac{21}{12}+\dfrac{8}{12}+\dfrac{58}{12}=\dfrac{87}{12}=7\tfrac{3}{12}=7\tfrac{1}{4}$ miles.

  4. Calculate
    a $2\tfrac{3}{5}+3\tfrac{1}{4}$b $8\tfrac{2}{3}+1\tfrac{5}{6}$c $5\tfrac{1}{2}-2\tfrac{4}{5}$d $4\tfrac{1}{8}-1\tfrac{5}{6}$

    a $5+\dfrac{12}{20}+\dfrac{5}{20}=5\tfrac{17}{20}$  b $9+\dfrac{4}{6}+\dfrac{5}{6}=9\tfrac{9}{6}=10\tfrac{1}{2}$  c $\dfrac{11}{2}-\dfrac{14}{5}=\dfrac{55}{10}-\dfrac{28}{10}=\dfrac{27}{10}=2\tfrac{7}{10}$  d $\dfrac{33}{8}-\dfrac{11}{6}=\dfrac{99}{24}-\dfrac{44}{24}=\dfrac{55}{24}=2\tfrac{7}{24}$.

  5. Evaluate, giving your answers in their simplest form: a $1\tfrac{1}{2}\times2\tfrac{2}{5}$   b $2\tfrac{2}{3}\times1\tfrac{1}{8}$

    Improper fractions first. a $\dfrac{3}{2}\times\dfrac{12}{5}=\dfrac{36}{10}=3\tfrac{3}{5}$  b $\dfrac{8}{3}\times\dfrac{9}{8}=3$.

  6. Write each recurring decimal as a fraction: a $0.\dot{6}$   b $0.\dot{3}\dot{6}$   c $0.5\dot{2}$

    a $x=0.666\ldots$, $10x=6.666\ldots$; subtract: $9x=6$, $x=\dfrac{2}{3}$.

    b $100x=36.3636\ldots$; subtract $x$: $99x=36$, $x=\dfrac{36}{99}=\dfrac{4}{11}$.

    c $10x=5.222\ldots$ and $100x=52.222\ldots$; subtract: $90x=47$, $x=\dfrac{47}{90}$.

  7. Find the side length of a cube with volume a $\dfrac{8}{125}$ m³   b $\dfrac{1}{64}$ cm³

    Side $=\sqrt[3]{\text{volume}}$: cube-root top and bottom. a $\dfrac{2}{5}$ m.  b $\dfrac{1}{4}$ cm.

Check: count decimal places when multiplying · make the divisor whole when dividing · mixed numbers → improper fractions before × or ÷ · simplest form at the end.

Shape and Space 10 — Graphs of sin, cos and tan · Sine rule · Cosine rule · Area of a triangle

Exercises

Calculator allowed. Marks in brackets. ★ = harder question. Lengths to 3 s.f., angles to 1 d.p. Diagrams not drawn accurately.

  1. [7]a Sketch the graph of $y=\cos\theta$ for $0^\circ\le\theta\le360^\circ$. b Use your graph to find all solutions of $\cos\theta=\dfrac12$ in that range.

    a Starts at 1, falls through 0 at $90^\circ$ to $-1$ at $180^\circ$, back through 0 at $270^\circ$ to 1 at $360^\circ$.

    b The line $y=\tfrac12$ meets the curve twice: $\theta=60^\circ$ (from the calculator) and, by the symmetry of the cosine graph, $\theta=360^\circ-60^\circ=300^\circ$.

  2. [6]In triangle ABC, angle A $=50^\circ$, AB $=9$ cm and AC $=6$ cm. Find a the length $a=$ BC  b angle B.

    a Two sides and the included angle → cosine rule: $a^2=9^2+6^2-2\times9\times6\cos50^\circ=47.58$, so $a=6.90$ cm.

    b Now a side and its opposite angle are known → sine rule: $\dfrac{\sin B}{6}=\dfrac{\sin50^\circ}{6.90}\Rightarrow\sin B=0.6663\Rightarrow B=41.8^\circ$.

  3. [6]In triangle ABC, angle B $=75^\circ$, AB $=14$ cm and BC $=19$ cm. Find a angle C  b the area of the triangle.

    a First AC by the cosine rule: $b^2=14^2+19^2-2\times14\times19\cos75^\circ=419.3$, $b=20.48$ cm. Then the sine rule: $\dfrac{\sin C}{14}=\dfrac{\sin75^\circ}{20.48}\Rightarrow\sin C=0.6604\Rightarrow C=41.3^\circ$.

    b Area $=\tfrac12\times14\times19\times\sin75^\circ=128.5$ cm² (half the product of two sides and the sine of the angle between them).

  4. [6]Town B is 20 km from town A on a bearing of 052°. Town C is 15 km from B on a bearing of 130°. a Calculate the direct distance AC. b Calculate the bearing of C from A.

    a The angle at B inside the triangle: the bearing of A from B is $052^\circ+180^\circ=232^\circ$, so angle ABC $=232^\circ-130^\circ=102^\circ$. Cosine rule: $\text{AC}^2=20^2+15^2-2\times20\times15\cos102^\circ=749.7$, $\text{AC}=27.4$ km.

    b Sine rule for angle BAC: $\dfrac{\sin A}{15}=\dfrac{\sin102^\circ}{27.38}\Rightarrow A=32.4^\circ$. Bearing of C from A $=052^\circ+32.4^\circ=84^\circ$ (3 figures: $084^\circ$).

Total: 25 marks

Check: sine graph starts at 0, cosine at 1; both repeat every 360° · second solution: sin → $180^\circ-\theta$, cos → $360^\circ-\theta$ · two sides + included angle, or three sides → cosine rule; otherwise sine rule · area $=\frac12ab\sin C$ · bearings: find the angle inside the triangle first, using north lines and $180^\circ$ back-bearings.

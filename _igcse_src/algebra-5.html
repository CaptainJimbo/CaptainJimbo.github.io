Algebra 5 — Multiplying brackets · Factorising quadratics · Quadratic equations

Exercises

No calculator. Marks in brackets. ★ = harder question. Check factorisations by expanding, and solutions by substituting.

  1. [4]Expand and simplify:
    a $(x+3)(x-5)$b $(x-6)^2$c $(x-1)(x+2)(x-3)$

    a FOIL: $x^2-5x+3x-15=x^2-2x-15$.

    b $(x-6)(x-6)=x^2-12x+36$ — not $x^2+36$.

    c First two: $(x-1)(x+2)=x^2+x-2$. Then $(x^2+x-2)(x-3)=x^3-3x^2+x^2-3x-2x+6=x^3-2x^2-5x+6$.

  2. [5]Factorise:
    a $x^2-4x$b $x^2-49$c $x^2-7x+10$

    a Common factor: $x(x-4)$.  b Difference of two squares: $(x-7)(x+7)$.  c Two numbers that multiply to $+10$ and add to $-7$: $-2$ and $-5$: $(x-2)(x-5)$.

  3. [8]Factorise and solve for $x$:
    a $x^2+5x+6=0$b $x^2-3x=10$c $x^2+4x=0$d $x^2-9=0$

    a $(x+2)(x+3)=0 \Rightarrow x=-2$ or $x=-3$.

    b Rearrange to $=0$ first: $x^2-3x-10=0 \Rightarrow (x-5)(x+2)=0 \Rightarrow x=5$ or $x=-2$.

    c $x(x+4)=0 \Rightarrow x=0$ or $x=-4$ (don't lose the $x=0$ solution by dividing by $x$).

    d $(x-3)(x+3)=0 \Rightarrow x=\pm3$.

  4. [4]A triangle has base $2x$ cm and height $(x+3)$ cm.
    • a Find and simplify an expression for its area.
    • b The area is 40 cm². Find $x$.

    a $\tfrac{1}{2}\times2x\times(x+3)=x(x+3)=x^2+3x$.

    b $x^2+3x=40 \Rightarrow x^2+3x-40=0 \Rightarrow (x+8)(x-5)=0$. $x=-8$ is impossible for a length, so $x=5$. Check: base 10, height 8, area 40 ✓

  5. [4]A rectangle $(2x+1)$ cm by $(x+2)$ cm has a square of side $x$ cm cut out of it.
    • a Find and simplify an expression for the remaining (shaded) area.
    • b The shaded area is 16 cm². Find the dimensions of the rectangle.

    a $(2x+1)(x+2)-x^2=2x^2+5x+2-x^2=x^2+5x+2$.

    b $x^2+5x+2=16 \Rightarrow x^2+5x-14=0 \Rightarrow (x+7)(x-2)=0 \Rightarrow x=2$ (positive). Rectangle $5$ cm by $4$ cm (check: $20-4=16$ ✓).

  6. [3]Two consecutive positive integers have a product of 132. Form a quadratic equation and find the integers.

    $n(n+1)=132 \Rightarrow n^2+n-132=0 \Rightarrow (n+12)(n-11)=0 \Rightarrow n=11$ (positive). The integers are 11 and 12.

Total: 28 marks

Check: FOIL — First, Outside, Inside, Last · $(x-a)^2$ has a middle term · to solve, get "$=0$" first, factorise, then set each bracket to zero · reject solutions that don't fit the context (negative lengths).

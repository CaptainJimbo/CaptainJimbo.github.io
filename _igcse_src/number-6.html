Number 6 — Direct and inverse proportion · Fractional and negative indices

Exercises

No calculator for questions 4 and 5. Marks in brackets. ★ = harder question.

  1. [4]The cost of copper wire is directly proportional to its length. A 2.5 m length costs £4.50.
    a Find the cost of 7 m of wire.b What length can be bought for £27?c Write a formula connecting the cost $C$ (in £) and the length $L$ (in m).

    Find the cost of 1 m first: $4.50\div2.5=£1.80$ per metre. a $7\times1.80=£12.60$.  b $27\div1.80=15$ m.  c $C=1.8L$.

  2. [4]The time taken for a journey is inversely proportional to the average speed. At an average speed of 60 km/h the journey takes 40 minutes.
    a How long does it take at 80 km/h?b What average speed is needed to complete it in 32 minutes?

    Inverse proportion: the product speed × time is constant: $60\times40=2400$. a $2400\div80=30$ minutes.  b $2400\div32=75$ km/h. (Check: faster speed, shorter time ✓.)

  3. [4]A tank can be emptied by 4 identical pumps in 9 hours. The time taken is inversely proportional to the number of pumps working.
    a How long would 6 pumps take?b How many pumps are needed to empty the tank in 4.5 hours?c Sketch the graph of time against number of pumps.

    Pumps × hours is constant: $4\times9=36$. a $36\div6=6$ hours.  b $36\div4.5=8$ pumps.  c A reciprocal curve: it falls steeply at first and flattens out, never touching either axis (time $=36/n$).

  4. [9]Work out, without a calculator. Give exact answers.
    a $49^{\frac{1}{2}}$b $27^{\frac{2}{3}}$c $16^{-\frac{1}{2}}$d $\left(\dfrac{25}{9}\right)^{\frac{3}{2}}$e $5^{-2}$
    f $\left(\dfrac{2}{3}\right)^{-3}$g $8^{-\frac{2}{3}}$h $\left(1\dfrac{7}{9}\right)^{\frac{1}{2}}$i $1\div81^{-\frac{1}{4}}$

    Root first (the denominator of the index), then the power (the numerator); a negative index means "one over".

    a $\sqrt{49}=7$.  b $(\sqrt[3]{27})^2=3^2=9$.  c $\dfrac{1}{\sqrt{16}}=\dfrac{1}{4}$.  d $\left(\sqrt{\tfrac{25}{9}}\right)^3=\left(\tfrac{5}{3}\right)^3=\dfrac{125}{27}$.  e $\dfrac{1}{5^2}=\dfrac{1}{25}$.

    f Flip the fraction, then cube: $\left(\tfrac{3}{2}\right)^3=\dfrac{27}{8}$.  g $\dfrac{1}{(\sqrt[3]{8})^2}=\dfrac{1}{4}$.  h $1\tfrac{7}{9}=\tfrac{16}{9}$, so $\sqrt{\tfrac{16}{9}}=\dfrac{4}{3}$.  i $81^{-\frac{1}{4}}=\dfrac{1}{3}$, and $1\div\tfrac{1}{3}=3$.

  5. [4]Solve for $x$, without a calculator.
    a $\left(\dfrac{4}{9}\right)^{x}=\dfrac{27}{8}$b $125^{x}=\dfrac{1}{25}$

    Write both sides as powers of the same base, then equate the indices.

    a $\tfrac{4}{9}=\left(\tfrac{2}{3}\right)^2$ and $\tfrac{27}{8}=\left(\tfrac{3}{2}\right)^3=\left(\tfrac{2}{3}\right)^{-3}$. So $\left(\tfrac{2}{3}\right)^{2x}=\left(\tfrac{2}{3}\right)^{-3}\Rightarrow 2x=-3\Rightarrow x=-\tfrac{3}{2}$.

    b $125=5^3$ and $\tfrac{1}{25}=5^{-2}$. So $5^{3x}=5^{-2}\Rightarrow 3x=-2\Rightarrow x=-\tfrac{2}{3}$.

Total: 25 marks

Check: direct proportion — find the value for 1 unit (or keep the ratio constant) · inverse proportion — the product is constant · $a^{\frac{m}{n}}=(\sqrt[n]{a})^m$: root first · $a^{-n}=\dfrac{1}{a^n}$ and $\left(\dfrac{a}{b}\right)^{-n}=\left(\dfrac{b}{a}\right)^{n}$ · to solve $a^{f(x)}=a^{g(x)}$, equate the indices.

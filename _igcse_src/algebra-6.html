Algebra 6 — Proportion with a constant $k$ · Laws of indices

Exercises

Calculator allowed except in question 6. Marks in brackets. ★ = harder question.

  1. [3]$y$ is directly proportional to $x$. When $x=8$, $y=12$.
    a Find a formula for $y$ in terms of $x$.b Find $y$ when $x=10$.c Find $x$ when $y=4.5$.

    a $y=kx$: $12=8k\Rightarrow k=1.5$, so $y=1.5x$.  b $y=1.5\times10=15$.  c $4.5=1.5x\Rightarrow x=3$.

  2. [4]$y$ is directly proportional to the square of $x$. When $x=3$, $y=45$.
    a Find a formula for $y$ in terms of $x$.b Find $y$ when $x=4$.c Find the positive value of $x$ when $y=125$.

    a $y=kx^2$: $45=9k\Rightarrow k=5$, so $y=5x^2$.  b $y=5\times16=80$.  c $125=5x^2\Rightarrow x^2=25\Rightarrow x=5$.

  3. [4]$y$ is directly proportional to $\sqrt{x}$. When $x=9$, $y=6$.
    a Find a formula for $y$ in terms of $x$.b Find $y$ when $x=25$.c Find $x$ when $y=1$.

    a $y=k\sqrt{x}$: $6=3k\Rightarrow k=2$, so $y=2\sqrt{x}$.  b $y=2\times5=10$.  c $1=2\sqrt{x}\Rightarrow\sqrt{x}=\tfrac{1}{2}\Rightarrow x=\tfrac{1}{4}$.

  4. [6]The brightness $I$ of a lamp, measured at a distance $d$ metres from it, is inversely proportional to the square of the distance. At 2 m the brightness is 36 units.
    a Find a formula for $I$ in terms of $d$.b Find the brightness at 6 m.
    c At what distance is the brightness 9 units?d The distance is doubled. What happens to the brightness? Explain.

    a $I=\dfrac{k}{d^2}$: $36=\dfrac{k}{4}\Rightarrow k=144$, so $I=\dfrac{144}{d^2}$.  b $I=\dfrac{144}{36}=4$ units.  c $9=\dfrac{144}{d^2}\Rightarrow d^2=16\Rightarrow d=4$ m.

    d Replacing $d$ by $2d$ gives $\dfrac{144}{(2d)^2}=\dfrac{144}{4d^2}=\dfrac{1}{4}\times\dfrac{144}{d^2}$: the brightness is divided by 4.

  5. [4]A fixed amount of gas is squeezed in a cylinder. Its pressure $P$ (in kPa) is inversely proportional to its volume $V$ (in cm³). When $V=240$, $P=125$.
    a Find a formula for $P$ in terms of $V$.b Find $P$ when $V=150$.c Find $V$ when $P=400$.

    a $P=\dfrac{k}{V}$: $125=\dfrac{k}{240}\Rightarrow k=30\,000$, so $P=\dfrac{30\,000}{V}$.  b $P=30\,000\div150=200$ kPa.  c $V=30\,000\div400=75$ cm³.

  6. [4]Simplify, without a calculator.
    a $2a^{-3}\times5a^{5}$b $\left(c^{-6}\right)^{\frac{1}{3}}$c $d^{\frac{3}{2}}\times\left(d^{\frac{1}{2}}\right)^{-3}\div d^{-1}$d Find $x$ if $\left(\dfrac{27}{8}\right)^{x}=\dfrac{4}{9}$.

    a $2\times5\times a^{-3+5}=10a^{2}$.  b $c^{-6\times\frac{1}{3}}=c^{-2}=\dfrac{1}{c^{2}}$.  c $d^{\frac{3}{2}}\times d^{-\frac{3}{2}}\div d^{-1}=d^{0-(-1)}=d$.

    d $\tfrac{27}{8}=\left(\tfrac{3}{2}\right)^3$ and $\tfrac{4}{9}=\left(\tfrac{2}{3}\right)^2=\left(\tfrac{3}{2}\right)^{-2}$, so $3x=-2\Rightarrow x=-\tfrac{2}{3}$.

Total: 25 marks

Check: "$y$ varies as $x^2$" means $y=kx^2$ — always find $k$ first from the given pair · inverse: $y=\dfrac{k}{x}$, $\dfrac{k}{x^2}$, $\dfrac{k}{\sqrt{x}}$ · multiply → add indices, divide → subtract, power of a power → multiply · $a^0=1$, $a^{-n}=\dfrac{1}{a^n}$, $a^{\frac{m}{n}}=(\sqrt[n]{a})^m$.

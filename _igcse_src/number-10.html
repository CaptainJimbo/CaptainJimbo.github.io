Number 10 — Rational and irrational numbers · Surds

Exercises

No calculator. Marks in brackets. ★ = harder question. Leave answers as exact surds in their simplest form.

  1. [4]Simplify
    a $\sqrt{80}-\sqrt{20}$b $\sqrt{\dfrac{81}{49}}$

    a $\sqrt{80}=\sqrt{16\times5}=4\sqrt5$ and $\sqrt{20}=2\sqrt5$, so $4\sqrt5-2\sqrt5=2\sqrt5$.  b $\dfrac{\sqrt{81}}{\sqrt{49}}=\dfrac{9}{7}$.

  2. [4]Simplify
    a $\sqrt{18}+3\sqrt{8}$b $2\sqrt{75}-\sqrt{27}$

    a $3\sqrt2+3\times2\sqrt2=3\sqrt2+6\sqrt2=9\sqrt2$.  b $2\times5\sqrt3-3\sqrt3=10\sqrt3-3\sqrt3=7\sqrt3$.

  3. [4]Expand and simplify
    a $(3-\sqrt2)^2$b $(2+\sqrt5)(2-\sqrt5)$

    a $9-6\sqrt2+2=11-6\sqrt2$.  b Difference of two squares: $4-5=-1$ (the surd terms cancel).

  4. [4]Rationalise the denominator and simplify
    a $\dfrac{2+\sqrt3}{\sqrt3}$b $\dfrac{5}{3-\sqrt7}$

    a Multiply top and bottom by $\sqrt3$: $\dfrac{2\sqrt3+3}{3}$.  b Multiply top and bottom by $3+\sqrt7$: $\dfrac{5(3+\sqrt7)}{9-7}=\dfrac{5(3+\sqrt7)}{2}=\dfrac{15+5\sqrt7}{2}$.

  5. [4]Work out, giving each answer in the form $\dfrac{a\sqrt2}{b}$:
    a $\dfrac{5}{\sqrt2}+\dfrac{3}{\sqrt8}$b $\dfrac{10}{\sqrt{50}}+\dfrac{3}{\sqrt2}$

    a $\sqrt8=2\sqrt2$, so $\dfrac{5}{\sqrt2}+\dfrac{3}{2\sqrt2}=\dfrac{10+3}{2\sqrt2}=\dfrac{13}{2\sqrt2}=\dfrac{13\sqrt2}{4}$.  b $\sqrt{50}=5\sqrt2$, so $\dfrac{10}{5\sqrt2}+\dfrac{3}{\sqrt2}=\dfrac{2}{\sqrt2}+\dfrac{3}{\sqrt2}=\dfrac{5}{\sqrt2}=\dfrac{5\sqrt2}{2}$.

  6. [2]The diagram shows an isosceles right-angled triangle with two sides of length 1. a Use it to write $\sin45^\circ$ as a surd in its simplest form. b A boat sails 24 km on a bearing of 045°. How far east has it travelled? Give an exact answer.

    a Hypotenuse $=\sqrt{1+1}=\sqrt2$, so $\sin45^\circ=\dfrac{1}{\sqrt2}=\dfrac{\sqrt2}{2}$.  b East $=24\sin45^\circ=24\times\dfrac{\sqrt2}{2}=12\sqrt2$ km.

  7. [3]A right-angled triangle has shorter sides $\sqrt3+1$ and $\sqrt3-1$. Find the exact length $h$ of the hypotenuse.

    Pythagoras: $h^2=(\sqrt3+1)^2+(\sqrt3-1)^2=(3+2\sqrt3+1)+(3-2\sqrt3+1)=8$, so $h=\sqrt8=2\sqrt2$.

Total: 25 marks

Check: $\sqrt{ab}=\sqrt a\sqrt b$ — pull out the largest square factor · add/subtract only like surds · $(a+\sqrt b)(a-\sqrt b)=a^2-b$ · rationalise $\frac{1}{\sqrt b}$ with $\sqrt b$, and $\frac{1}{a\pm\sqrt b}$ with $a\mp\sqrt b$ · a surd answer is exact; a decimal is not.

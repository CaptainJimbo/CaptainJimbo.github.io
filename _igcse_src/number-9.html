Number 9 — Comparative costs · Taxation · Salaries and income tax · Foreign currency

Exercises

Calculator allowed. Marks in brackets. ★ = harder question. Money answers to the nearest cent/penny unless told otherwise.

  1. [4]Which car has the better fuel efficiency? Show your working.
    carfuel useddistance
    Vega9.5 litres150 km
    Orion13.2 litres220 km

    Compare kilometres per litre: Vega $150\div9.5=15.79$ km/l, Orion $220\div13.2=16.67$ km/l. Orion travels further on each litre, so Orion is more efficient. (Litres per 100 km gives the same conclusion: 6.33 vs 6.00.)

  2. [3]A phone costs £552 including sales tax at 20%. Calculate the price before tax.

    The price with tax is $120\%$ of the original, so original $=552\div1.2=£460.00$. (Not $552-20\%$ of 552, which would give £441.60.)

  3. [8]The country of Numeria has these income-tax rates.
    tax rateannual salary
    0%first \$10 000
    20%\$10 001 to \$40 000
    40%above \$40 000
    Mr Costa earns \$36 000 a year and Mrs Costa earns \$58 000 a year. How much tax does the family pay per week?

    Each band is taxed only on the money inside it.

    Mr Costa: $0$ on the first $10\,000$; $20\%$ of the remaining $26\,000=\$5\,200$.

    Mrs Costa: $0$ on $10\,000$; $20\%$ of the next $30\,000=\$6000$; $40\%$ of the $18\,000$ above $40\,000=\$7200$. Total $\$13\,200$.

    Family per year $5200+13\,200=\$18\,400$; per week $18\,400\div52=\$353.85$.

  4. [6]£1 (UK pounds) $=\$1.28$ (US dollars) $=\text{€}1.17$ (euros). Convert
    a £40 into dollarsb \$40 into poundsc €40 into dollars.

    a $40\times1.28=\$51.20$.  b $40\div1.28=£31.25$.  c Go through pounds: $40\div1.17=£34.19$, then $\times1.28=\$43.76$.

  5. [4]Nadia wants to buy a flat in Spain costing €240 000 using UK pounds. In March the exchange rate was £1 $=\text{€}1.15$; in June it was £1 $=\text{€}1.20$. How much would she save, in pounds, by buying in June rather than March?

    March: $240\,000\div1.15=£208\,695.65$. June: $240\,000\div1.20=£200\,000$. Saving $=£8\,695.65$ — a stronger pound buys more euros, so the same euro price costs fewer pounds.

Total: 25 marks

Check: best value = compare per unit (per litre, per 100 g, km per litre) · a price including tax is (100 + rate)% of the original — divide, don't subtract · tax bands: only the slice inside each band is taxed at that rate · currency: multiply to go from the £1 side, divide to come back · convert via the common currency when neither is £.

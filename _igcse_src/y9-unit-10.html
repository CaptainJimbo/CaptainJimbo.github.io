  2. Write each equation in the form $y=mx+c$. Then write down the gradient and the $y$-intercept.
    a$y-3x=8$b$y+\tfrac12x=5$c$2y=6x-10$d$3y+2x=12$

    a $y=3x+8$: gradient $3$, intercept $8$.   b $y=-\tfrac12x+5$: gradient $-\tfrac12$, intercept $5$.

    c $y=3x-5$: gradient $3$, intercept $-5$.   d $3y=-2x+12$, so $y=-\tfrac23x+4$: gradient $-\tfrac23$, intercept $4$.

  3. Work out the equation of
    • a the line through $(2,5)$ and $(6,17)$
    • b the line with gradient $-2$ that passes through $(1,4)$.

    a Gradient $=\dfrac{17-5}{6-2}=3$. Substituting $(2,5)$: $5=6+c$, so $c=-1$. Equation $y=3x-1$.

    b $y=-2x+c$; $4=-2+c$, so $c=6$. Equation $y=-2x+6$.

  4. Here are four equations:   A $y=2x-5$   B $2y-4x=7$   C $3y+2x=-6$   D $y=4x+5$
    • a Which two lines are parallel?
    • b Which line is the steepest?
    • c Which line passes through $(0,-2)$?

    Rearranged: B is $y=2x+3.5$ and C is $y=-\tfrac23x-2$.

    a A and B (both have gradient 2).   b D (largest gradient, 4).   c C (intercept $-2$).

  5. Solve each pair of simultaneous equations by substitution.
    a$7x+y=45$  and  $y=2x$b$x+4y=30$  and  $x=2y$

    a Replace $y$ with $2x$: $7x+2x=45$, $x=5$, $y=10$.

    b Replace $x$ with $2y$: $2y+4y=30$, $y=5$, $x=10$.

  6. Solve each pair of simultaneous equations by elimination.
    a$3x+y=23$
    $x-y=5$    b$5x+2y=31$
    $3x-2y=9$    c$x+6y=40$
    $x+2y=16$    d$5x+3y=42$
    $2x+3y=24$

    a Add: $4x=28$, $x=7$; then $y=2$.   b Add: $8x=40$, $x=5$; then $2y=6$, $y=3$.

    c Subtract: $4y=24$, $y=6$; then $x=4$.   d Subtract: $3x=18$, $x=6$; then $3y=12$, $y=4$.

  7. Solve.
    a$4x+y=27$
    $x+3y=26$    b$5x-y=13$
    $2x+3y=12$

    a Multiply the first by 3: $12x+3y=81$. Subtract $x+3y=26$: $11x=55$, $x=5$, $y=7$.

    b Multiply the first by 3: $15x-3y=39$. Add $2x+3y=12$: $17x=51$, $x=3$, $y=2$.

    • a Draw the graphs of $y=2x+1$ and $y=5x-5$ on the same axes, for values of $x$ from 0 to 4.
    • b Use your graphs to solve the simultaneous equations $y=2x+1$ and $y=5x-5$.

    a $y=2x+1$ passes through $(0,1)$, $(2,5)$, $(4,9)$. $y=5x-5$ passes through $(1,0)$, $(2,5)$, $(3,10)$.

    b The lines cross at $(2,5)$, so $x=2$, $y=5$. Check: $2x+1=5x-5$ gives $3x=6$, $x=2$.

  9. At a cinema, 3 adult tickets and 2 child tickets cost €38. 1 adult ticket and 4 child tickets cost €31.
    • a Write two equations, using $a$ for the price of an adult ticket and $c$ for the price of a child ticket.
    • b Solve them to find the price of each ticket.

    a $3a+2c=38$ and $a+4c=31$.

    b Multiply the first by 2: $6a+4c=76$. Subtract the second: $5a=45$, $a=9$. Then $4c=22$, $c=5.5$. An adult ticket costs €9 and a child ticket €5.50.

  10. A fairground charges an entrance fee and then a fixed price per ride. Ana went on 12 rides and paid €31 in total. Ben went on 5 rides and paid €17 in total. Work out the entrance fee and the price of a ride.

    Let $x$ be the price per ride and $y$ the entrance fee: $12x+y=31$ and $5x+y=17$. Subtract: $7x=14$, $x=2$; then $y=7$. Rides cost €2 each and the entrance fee is €7.

    • a Draw the graphs of $y=x^{2}-2$ and $y=x$ on the same axes, for values of $x$ from $-2$ to $3$.
    • b Write down the coordinates of the points where the graphs intersect.
    • c Use your answer to solve the equation $x^{2}-2=x$.

    a $y=x^{2}-2$ is a parabola through $(-2,2)$, $(-1,-1)$, $(0,-2)$, $(1,-1)$, $(2,2)$, $(3,7)$. $y=x$ is the straight line through $(0,0)$ and $(3,3)$.

    b $(-1,-1)$ and $(2,2)$.

    c $x=-1$ or $x=2$. (Check: $x^{2}-x-2=(x-2)(x+1)=0$.)

Check: in $y=mx+c$, $m$ is the gradient and $c$ the $y$-intercept · parallel lines have equal gradients · same signs subtract, different signs add · always check a pair of answers in both equations.

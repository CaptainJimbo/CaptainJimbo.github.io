Sets 1 — Set notation · Venn diagrams

Exercises

Notation: $\in$ is a member of · $\notin$ is not a member of · $n(A)$ number of elements · $\varnothing$ empty set · $\mathscr{E}$ universal set · $A'$ complement · $A\cap B$ intersection · $A\cup B$ union · $B\subset A$ subset. Marks in brackets. ★ = harder question.

  1. [4]Say whether each statement is true or false.
    • a $7\in\{\text{prime numbers}\}$
    • b $15\notin\{\text{multiples of 3}\}$
    • c square $\in\{\text{quadrilaterals}\}$
    • d $0.5\in\{\text{integers}\}$

    a True.  b False — $15=3\times5$ is a multiple of 3.  c True — a square has four sides.  d False — integers are whole numbers.

  2. [6]$\mathscr{E}=\{\text{integers from 1 to 12}\}$, $A=\{\text{even numbers}\}$, $B=\{\text{multiples of 3}\}$.
    • a List the set $A\cap B$.
    • b List the set $A\cup B$.
    • c Find $n(B')$.
    • d Describe the set $A\cap B$ in words.

    $A=\{2,4,6,8,10,12\}$, $B=\{3,6,9,12\}$.

    a In both: $A\cap B=\{6,12\}$.  b In either: $A\cup B=\{2,3,4,6,8,9,10,12\}$.  c $B'$ = everything not a multiple of 3: $12-4=8$ elements.  d The multiples of 6 (even and a multiple of 3).

  3. [5]In a class of 30 students, $F=\{\text{plays football}\}$ and $T=\{\text{plays tennis}\}$. 12 students play football only, 5 play both, and 8 play tennis only.
    • a Draw a Venn diagram showing this information.
    • b How many students play neither sport?
    • c Find $n(F\cup T)$.
    • d Find $n(T)$.

    b $30-(12+5+8)=5$ students play neither.

    c $n(F\cup T)=12+5+8=25$.  d $n(T)=5+8=13$ (everyone inside the $T$ circle).

  4. [4]$\mathscr{E}=\{\text{cars in a car park}\}$, $R=\{\text{red cars}\}$, $E=\{\text{electric cars}\}$. Describe in words:
    • a the set $R'$
    • b the set $R\cap E$
    • c the set $R\cup E$
    • d what it means if $R\cap E=\varnothing$.

    a The cars that are not red.  b The red electric cars.  c The cars that are red or electric (or both).  d There is no red electric car in the car park.

  5. [3]For two sets $P$ and $Q$: $n(\mathscr{E})=40$, $n(P)=22$, $n(Q)=19$ and $n(P\cap Q)=7$. Find $n(P\cup Q)$ and $n\big((P\cup Q)'\big)$.

    Adding $n(P)$ and $n(Q)$ counts the overlap twice: $n(P\cup Q)=22+19-7=34$. Outside both: $40-34=6$.

  6. [4]$\mathscr{E}=\{\text{positive integers up to 20}\}$, $M=\{\text{multiples of 4}\}$, $E=\{\text{even numbers}\}$.
    • a List $M$ and explain why $M\subset E$.
    • b Find $n(E\cap M')$ and describe this set.

    a $M=\{4,8,12,16,20\}$. Every multiple of 4 is even, so every member of $M$ is in $E$: $M$ is a subset of $E$.

    b $E$ has 10 members; remove the 5 multiples of 4: $n(E\cap M')=5$ — the even numbers that are not multiples of 4: $\{2,6,10,14,18\}$.

Total: 26 marks

Check: $\cap$ = "and" (overlap), $\cup$ = "or" (everything inside), $'$ = "not" · don't double-count the overlap · the number outside the circles is part of $\mathscr{E}$ too.

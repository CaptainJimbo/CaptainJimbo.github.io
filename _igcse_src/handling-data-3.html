Handling Data 3 — Measures of dispersion · Quartiles · Cumulative frequency

Exercises

Quartiles for small data sets: $Q_1$ is the $\frac{1}{4}(n+1)$th value, the median the $\frac{1}{2}(n+1)$th, $Q_3$ the $\frac{3}{4}(n+1)$th — if that falls between two values, take their mean. Squared paper for the graph question. Marks in brackets. ★ = harder question.

  1. [6]The average number of rainy days per month in a city is: Jan 2, Feb 1, Mar 3, Apr 4, May 15, Jun 24, Jul 27, Aug 26, Sep 20, Oct 12, Nov 5, Dec 2. Calculate a the range, b the median, c the inter-quartile range.

    In order ($n=12$): 1, 2, 2, 3, 4, 5, 12, 15, 20, 24, 26, 27.

    a $27-1=26$.  b $\frac{13}{2}=6.5$th value: mean of 6th and 7th $=\dfrac{5+12}{2}=8.5$ days.

    c $Q_1$: $3.25$th → between 3rd and 4th: $\dfrac{2+3}{2}=2.5$. $Q_3$: $9.75$th → between 9th and 10th: $\dfrac{20+24}{2}=22$. IQR $=22-2.5=19.5$ days — the wet and dry seasons make the middle half very spread out.

  2. [6]Mira divides her garden into nine equal squares and counts the daisies in each: 10, 5, 15, 5, 10, 0, 10, 5, 15. Calculate a the range, b the median, c the inter-quartile range.

    In order ($n=9$): 0, 5, 5, 5, 10, 10, 10, 15, 15.

    a $15-0=15$.  b 5th value: $10$.  c $Q_1$ = 2.5th → $\dfrac{5+5}{2}=5$; $Q_3$ = 7.5th → $\dfrac{10+15}{2}=12.5$. IQR $=7.5$.

  3. [13]The marks of 30 boys and 30 girls in a test are grouped below.
    Marks $x$ (%)BoysGirls
    $031
    $2073
    $40108
    $60611
    $8047
    • a Draw a cumulative frequency graph for each set on the same axes, and use the graphs to estimate (i) the median and (ii) the inter-quartile range of each.
    • b Compare the performance of the boys and the girls.

    a Cumulative frequencies (plotted at the upper class boundaries 20, 40, 60, 80, 100): boys 3, 10, 20, 26, 30; girls 1, 4, 12, 23, 30. Join each set with a smooth S-shaped curve from $(0,0)$.

    Read across at $\tfrac{1}{2}n=15$ for the median, $7.5$ for $Q_1$ and $22.5$ for $Q_3$. Boys: median $\approx50$, $Q_1\approx33$, $Q_3\approx68$, IQR $\approx35$. Girls: median $\approx65$, $Q_1\approx49$, $Q_3\approx79$, IQR $\approx30$. (Accept readings within a few marks.)

    b The girls did better on average — their median is about 15 marks higher. Their IQR is slightly smaller, so their marks are a little more consistent; the boys' marks are more spread out.

Total: 25 marks

Check: order the data first · the IQR measures the spread of the middle 50% and ignores extremes · cumulative frequency is plotted at the end of each class · for large $n$ read the quartiles at $\frac{1}{4}n$, $\frac{1}{2}n$, $\frac{3}{4}n$ · compare with an average and a spread.

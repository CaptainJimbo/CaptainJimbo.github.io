A · Percentages

  1. Copy and complete the table of equivalents.
    Fraction$\frac{7}{20}$$2\frac{2}{5}$
    Decimal0.61.25
    Percentage15%

    $0.6=\dfrac{3}{5}=60\%$; $\dfrac{7}{20}=0.35=35\%$; $15\%=0.15=\dfrac{3}{20}$; $1.25=1\tfrac{1}{4}=125\%$; $2\tfrac{2}{5}=2.4=240\%$.

  2. Three groups of students tried a new snack. Group A: 80 of 200 liked it. Group B: 33 of 60. Group C: 63 of 140.
    • a Write each group's proportion who liked it as a percentage.
    • b Which group liked it most? Which group liked it least?

    a A $\dfrac{80}{200}=40\%$, B $\dfrac{33}{60}=55\%$, C $\dfrac{63}{140}=45\%$.

    b Most: B. Least: A — percentages let you compare groups of different sizes.

  3. A 750 ml fruit drink contains 300 ml of apple juice, 150 ml of mango juice and the rest is grape juice. What percentage of the drink is a apple, b mango, c grape?

    a $\dfrac{300}{750}=40\%$  b $\dfrac{150}{750}=20\%$  c $100-40-20=40\%$ (300 ml).

  4. A phone costs €480. In a sale it is reduced by 35%. Work out the sale price.

    Multiplier for $-35\%$ is $0.65$: $480\times0.65=€312$. (Or $35\%$ of 480 is 168; $480-168=312$.)

  5. Omar gets a pay rise of 6%. His old salary was €25 000. What is his new salary?

    $25\,000\times1.06=€26\,500$.

    • a 10% of an amount is 4.8 kg. Work out the amount.
    • b 30% of an amount is 72 m. Work out the amount.

    a $10\%\to4.8$, so $100\%\to48$ kg.  b $30\%\to72$, so $10\%\to24$ and $100\%\to240$ m.

  7. calc Lena invests €2400 for 3 years at 4.5% simple interest per year. How much is the investment worth after 3 years?

    Interest each year $=0.045\times2400=€108$; over 3 years $€324$. Total $2400+324=€2724$.

  8. calc Kostas puts €3000 in an account paying 2% compound interest per year. How much is in the account after 3 years?

    Multiply by $1.02$ three times: $3000\times1.02^3=3000\times1.061208=€3183.62$ (to the nearest cent). Each year the interest is earned on a slightly bigger amount.

B · Ratios

  1. Simplify each ratio.
    a $14:21$b $2.5:7.5$c $0.8:2$

    a $2:3$ (÷7).  b $1:3$ (÷2.5).  c $\times10$ first: $8:20=2:5$.

  2. Sophie mixes squash and water in the ratio $3:7$. She makes 450 ml of drink. How much squash and how much water does she use?

    $3+7=10$ parts; one part $=450\div10=45$ ml. Squash $3\times45=135$ ml, water $7\times45=315$ ml (check: 450 ✓).

  3. Green paint is made from blue, yellow and white in the ratio $5:3:2$. How much of each colour is needed for 2 litres of green paint?

    10 parts; $2000\div10=200$ ml per part. Blue $1000$ ml, yellow $600$ ml, white $400$ ml.

  4. An alloy is 60% copper, 30% zinc and 10% nickel.
    • a Write the fraction of the alloy that is each metal, in simplest form.
    • b Write the ratio copper : zinc : nickel in its simplest form.

    a Copper $\dfrac{3}{5}$, zinc $\dfrac{3}{10}$, nickel $\dfrac{1}{10}$.  b $60:30:10=6:3:1$.

  5. A shop has 20% off everything. For one day it offers an extra 30% off the sale price.
    • a A jacket originally costs €150. Work out its price on that day.
    • b Is "20% off then 30% off" the same as 50% off? Explain.

    a $150\times0.8=120$, then $120\times0.7=€84$.

    b No. The multipliers combine: $0.8\times0.7=0.56$, so you pay 56% — a 44% discount, not 50%. The second 30% is taken off a smaller amount.

  6. After a 15% reduction, a pair of trainers costs €51. What was the original price?

    $€51$ is $85\%$ of the original: $1\%\to0.60$, so $100\%\to€60$. Check: $60\times0.85=51$ ✓

Check: percentage = part ÷ whole × 100 · multipliers: +6% → 1.06, −35% → 0.65 · simple interest is the same every year, compound grows · share in a ratio: find one part first.

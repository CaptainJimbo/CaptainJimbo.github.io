Exercises

No calculator. Marks in brackets. ★ = harder question. Check every solution by substitution.

A · Simplify as much as possible

  1. [1]$\dfrac{15x^2y}{5x}$

    $15\div5=3$, $x^2\div x=x$: $3xy$.

  2. [1]$(8ab^2)\div(12a^2b)$

    $\dfrac{8ab^2}{12a^2b}=\dfrac{2b}{3a}$ (cancel 4, $a$ and $b$).

  3. [1]$\dfrac{a}{b^2}\times\dfrac{b^3}{c}\times\dfrac{c^2}{a}$

    Cancel $a$ with $a$, $b^2$ with $b^3$ (leaving $b$), $c$ with $c^2$ (leaving $c$): $bc$.

  4. [1]$\dfrac{4x^2}{y}\div\dfrac{x}{y^2}$

    Multiply by the reciprocal: $\dfrac{4x^2}{y}\times\dfrac{y^2}{x}=4xy$.

  5. [2]$\dfrac{x}{3}-\dfrac{x}{5}$

    Common denominator 15: $\dfrac{5x}{15}-\dfrac{3x}{15}=\dfrac{2x}{15}$.

  6. [2]$\dfrac{x}{4}+\dfrac{3x}{8}$

    Common denominator 8: $\dfrac{2x}{8}+\dfrac{3x}{8}=\dfrac{5x}{8}$.

B · Equations with powers and roots

  1. [3]Solve $3x^2-5=70$

    Add 5: $3x^2=75$. Divide by 3: $x^2=25$. Square root — two answers: $x=5$ or $x=-5$. Check: $3\times25-5=70$ ✓ for both.

  2. [3]Solve $\dfrac{x^2-6}{2}=15$

    Multiply by 2: $x^2-6=30$. Add 6: $x^2=36$, so $x=\pm6$.

  3. [2]Solve $\dfrac{\sqrt{x-3}}{2}=2$

    Multiply by 2: $\sqrt{x-3}=4$. Square both sides: $x-3=16$, so $x=19$. Check: $\sqrt{16}\div2=2$ ✓ (one answer only — $\sqrt{\ }$ means the positive root).

D · Inequalities

  1. [3]Solve the inequality $12\le9-x$ and show the solution on a number line.

    Add $x$ to both sides: $12+x\le9$. Subtract 12: $x\le-3$. Number line: a solid circle at $-3$ (included) with the arrow pointing left.

  2. [3]List the integer solutions of $\;2\le2x

    Split into two: $2\le2x \Rightarrow x\ge1$; and $2x1, 2, 3, 4, 5, 6 (7 is not included).

  3. [2]Solve $3(x-2)>5(x-4)$

    Expand: $3x-6>5x-20$. Subtract $3x$: $-6>2x-20$. Add 20: $14>2x$, so $x<7$.

  4. [2]Solve $-2x+5\ge11$

    Subtract 5: $-2x\ge6$. Divide by $-2$ — dividing by a negative reverses the sign: $x\le-3$. Check with $x=-4$: $8+5=13\ge11$ ✓; with $x=0$: $5\ge11$ ✗.

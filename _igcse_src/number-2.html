Number 2 — Standard form · Percentages · Percentage change

Exercises

Calculator allowed. Give answers in standard form where the question is in standard form. Marks in brackets. ★ = harder question.

A · Standard form

  1. [4]Write in standard form:
    a $6\,800$b 42 millionc $0.000\,31$d three eighths

    A number between 1 and 10, times a power of 10. a $6.8\times10^3$  b $4.2\times10^7$  c $3.1\times10^{-4}$ (the point moves 4 places)  d $0.375=3.75\times10^{-1}$.

  2. [4]Write as ordinary numbers:
    a $2.7\times10^4$b $9.05\times10^6$c $6\times10^{-2}$d $1.8\times10^{-5}$

    a $27\,000$  b $9\,050\,000$  c $0.06$  d $0.000\,018$.

  3. [6]Work out, giving each answer in standard form (3 s.f. where needed):
    a $(3.2\times10^3)\times(2.5\times10^4)$b $\dfrac{8.4\times10^7}{2.1\times10^2}$c $6.5\times10^5+6.5\times10^4$d $(4\times10^{-3})^2$

    a Numbers and powers separately: $3.2\times2.5=8$, $10^{3+4}=10^7$ → $8\times10^7$.

    b $8.4\div2.1=4$, $10^{7-2}=10^5$ → $4\times10^5$.

    c Same power first: $6.5\times10^5+0.65\times10^5=7.15\times10^5$.

    d $4^2\times10^{-6}=16\times10^{-6}=1.6\times10^{-5}$ (16 is not between 1 and 10, so move the point).

  4. [3]Light travels at $3\times10^8$ m/s. The Sun is $1.5\times10^{11}$ m from the Earth. How long does sunlight take to reach us? Give your answer in minutes and seconds.

    time $=\dfrac{\text{distance}}{\text{speed}}=\dfrac{1.5\times10^{11}}{3\times10^8}=0.5\times10^3=500$ s $=8$ min $20$ s.

B · Percentages

  1. [2]About 60% of the human body is water. How many kilograms of water are in a person of mass 65 kg?

    $60\%$ of $65=0.6\times65=39$ kg.

  2. [4]A laptop costs €840 before VAT. VAT is 24%.
    • a Work out the price including VAT.
    • b In a sale, the price including VAT is reduced by 15%. Work out the sale price.

    a Multiplier for $+24\%$ is $1.24$: $840\times1.24=€1\,041.60$.

    b Multiplier for $-15\%$ is $0.85$: $1\,041.60\times0.85=€885.36$.

  3. [4]
    • a A town's population grew from $2.4\times10^5$ in 2010 to $2.64\times10^5$ in 2020. Find the percentage increase.
    • b The Earth is $1.496\times10^8$ km from the Sun and Mars is $2.279\times10^8$ km from the Sun. Write the Earth's distance as a percentage of the distance of Mars, to 3 s.f.

    a Percentage change $=\dfrac{\text{change}}{\text{original}}\times100=\dfrac{0.24\times10^5}{2.4\times10^5}\times100=10\%$.

    b $\dfrac{1.496\times10^8}{2.279\times10^8}\times100=65.6\%$ (3 s.f.).

  4. [2]The length of a rectangle is increased by 20% and its width is decreased by 20%. Find the percentage change in its area.

    Multipliers: $1.2\times0.8=0.96$, so the area is $96\%$ of the original — a $4\%$ decrease. (Try $10\times10$: it becomes $12\times8=96$.)

  5. [3]A marathon record of 2 hours 20 minutes was improved by 2.5%. Find the new record time in hours, minutes and seconds.

    $2$ h $20$ min $=140$ min. "Improved by 2.5%" means 2.5% less time: $140\times0.975=136.5$ min $=2$ h $16$ min $30$ s.

Total: 32 marks

Check: in standard form the first number is between 1 and 10 · same power of 10 before adding · percentage change is measured against the original · a multiplier below 1 means a decrease.

A · Negative integers

  1. The temperature in Oslo was $-6\,^\circ$C at 7 am and $3\,^\circ$C at 2 pm.
    • a Work out the rise in temperature between 7 am and 2 pm.
    • b By midnight the temperature had fallen by $11\,^\circ$C compared with 2 pm. What was the temperature at midnight?
    • c Work out $-3-7$.

    a From $-6$ up to $3$: $3-(-6)=3+6=9\,^\circ$C.

    b $3-11=-8\,^\circ$C.

    c Start at $-3$, go 7 further down: $-10$.

  2. Work out
    a $-4\times6$b $(-3)\times(-7)$c $45\div(-9)$d $-36\div(-4)$e $-2\times(-3)\times(-5)$

    Same signs → positive, different signs → negative. a $-24$  b $21$  c $-5$  d $9$  e $(-2)(-3)=6$, then $6\times(-5)=-30$ (three negatives → negative).

  3. Substitute the values into each formula and work out the answer.
    • a $v=u+at$ when $u=-5$, $a=3$ and $t=4$
    • b $A=2b-c^2$ when $b=-3$ and $c=-4$

    a $v=-5+3\times4=-5+12=7$.

    b $c^2=(-4)^2=16$, so $A=2\times(-3)-16=-6-16=-22$.

  4. Maya says that $(-5)^2=-25$. Explain her mistake and write the correct value.

    Squaring means multiplying the number by itself: $(-5)^2=(-5)\times(-5)=+25$ — two negatives give a positive. $-25$ would be $-5^2=-(5\times5)$, where only the 5 is squared.

B · Prime factor decomposition, HCF and LCM

  1. Write each number as a product of its prime factors, using index notation.
    a 84b 360

    a $84=2\times42=2\times2\times21=2\times2\times3\times7=2^2\times3\times7$.

    b $360=2\times180=2\times2\times90=2\times2\times2\times45=2^3\times3^2\times5$.

  2. Use your answers to question 5 to find
    a the HCF of 84 and 360b the LCM of 84 and 360

    $84=2^2\times3\times7$ and $360=2^3\times3^2\times5$.

    a HCF = the primes they share, lowest powers: $2^2\times3=12$.

    b LCM = every prime that appears, highest powers: $2^3\times3^2\times5\times7=2520$.

  3. Two buses leave a station at 9:00. Bus A leaves every 12 minutes and bus B every 18 minutes. At what time do they next leave together?

    They meet at multiples of both 12 and 18, i.e. the LCM: $12=2^2\times3$, $18=2\times3^2$, LCM $=2^2\times3^2=36$ minutes. Next together at 9:36.

C · Indices

  1. Write each calculation as a single power.
    a $7^4\times7^3$b $5^9\div5^4$c $(2^3)^4$d $3^6\times3\div3^2$e $10^8\div10^8$

    Multiply → add the indices, divide → subtract, power of a power → multiply.

    a $7^7$  b $5^5$  c $2^{12}$  d $3^{6+1-2}=3^5$  e $10^0=1$ (any number to the power 0 is 1).

  2. Evaluate $\dfrac{2^5\times2^3}{2^6}$

    $\dfrac{2^8}{2^6}=2^{8-6}=2^2=4$.

  3. Which calculation does not give the same answer as the others? Explain.
    A $(2\times3)^2$B $2\times3^2$C $2^2\times3^2$D $6^2$

    A $=6^2=36$, C $=4\times9=36$, D $=36$. B $=2\times9=18$ — the square applies only to the 3, because indices are done before multiplication. B.

D · Priority of operations

  1. Work out
    a $4+3\times2^2$b $(4+3)\times2^2$c $20-\sqrt{36}\div2$d $(10-2^3)^2\times3$e $\sqrt[3]{27}+5\times2$f $100\div(3+2)^2$

    a $4+3\times4=16$  b $7\times4=28$  c $20-6\div2=20-3=17$  d $(10-8)^2\times3=4\times3=12$  e $3+10=13$  f $100\div25=4$.

  2. Insert one pair of brackets to make this calculation correct: $\;3+5\times2-1=15$

    Without brackets: $3+10-1=12$. Forcing the addition first: $(3+5)\times2-1=16-1=15$ ✓.

  3. Evaluate $\dfrac{5\times2^4}{4\times10}$ and $\;\dfrac{(7-4)^2}{3^2-6}$

    $\dfrac{5\times16}{40}=\dfrac{80}{40}=2$.   $\dfrac{3^2}{9-6}=\dfrac{9}{3}=3$. (The fraction line acts like brackets: work out the top and the bottom first.)

Check: a sign for every negative answer · index laws only work for the same base · brackets → indices → ÷ × → + −.

Number 8 — Converting units of length, area and volume · Compound measures

Exercises

Calculator allowed. Marks in brackets. ★ = harder question. Give answers to 3 s.f. where rounding is needed.

  1. [2]A photograph measures 15 cm by 10 cm. Find its area in m².

    Convert the lengths first: $0.15\times0.10=0.015$ m². (Or $150$ cm² $\div10\,000=0.015$ m², since $1$ m² $=100^2$ cm².)

  2. [3]One litre is 1000 cm³. How many cubic millimetres are there in 2.5 litres? Give your answer in standard form.

    $2.5$ litres $=2500$ cm³. $1$ cm³ $=10^3$ mm³, so $2500\times10^3=2.5\times10^6$ mm³.

  3. [3]Sound travels at 340 m/s in air. a How many kilometres does sound travel in one day? b A cube has sides of that length. Work out its volume in km³, in standard form to 3 s.f.

    a One day $=24\times60\times60=86\,400$ s; distance $=340\times86\,400=29\,376\,000$ m $=2.94\times10^4$ km.  b $(2.9376\times10^4)^3=2.54\times10^{13}$ km³.

  4. [2]A person drinks about 730 litres of water a year. What is this in millilitres per minute?

    $730$ litres $=730\,000$ ml. Minutes in a year: $365\times24\times60=525\,600$. Rate $=730\,000\div525\,600=1.39$ ml per minute.

  5. [6]a A cyclist rides for 25 minutes at an average speed of 8 m/s. How far does she ride, in kilometres? b A sprinter runs 60 m in 7.5 s. What is his speed in km/h?

    a $25$ min $=1500$ s; distance $=8\times1500=12\,000$ m $=12$ km.  b $60\div7.5=8$ m/s. In one hour (3600 s) he would cover $8\times3600=28\,800$ m $=28.8$ km, so $28.8$ km/h.

  6. [6]a A brass ornament has density 8.5 g/cm³ and mass 1.7 kg. Find its volume. b A cubic metre of oak has a mass of 720 kg. Find the density of oak in g/cm³.

    a Density $=\dfrac{\text{mass}}{\text{volume}}$, so volume $=\dfrac{\text{mass}}{\text{density}}=\dfrac{1700}{8.5}=200$ cm³ (mass in grams to match g/cm³).  b $720$ kg $=720\,000$ g; $1$ m³ $=100^3=10^6$ cm³. Density $=\dfrac{720\,000}{1\,000\,000}=0.72$ g/cm³ (oak floats: less than water's 1 g/cm³).

  7. [3]Pressure $=\dfrac{\text{force}}{\text{area}}$. Copy and complete the table.
    forceareapressure
    60 N2.5 m²___ N/m²
    ___ N4.8 m²12.5 N/m²
    1500 N___ m²300 N/m²

    Row 1: $60\div2.5=24$ N/m². Row 2: force $=$ pressure $\times$ area $=12.5\times4.8=60$ N. Row 3: area $=$ force $\div$ pressure $=1500\div300=5$ m².

Total: 25 marks

Check: area units scale by the square of the length factor ($1$ m² $=10^4$ cm²), volume units by the cube ($1$ m³ $=10^6$ cm³ $=10^9$ mm³) · m/s → km/h: multiply by 3.6 · match units before dividing (g with cm³, kg with m³) · density = mass ÷ volume, pressure = force ÷ area — rearrange with a formula triangle.

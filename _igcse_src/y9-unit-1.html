Significant figures, powers and standard form — Estimating · Indices · Prefixes · Standard form

Exercises

Questions marked calc may be done with a calculator; do the rest without one. ★ = challenge question.

  1. Round each number to the accuracy shown.
    a$48\,762$ (2 s.f.)b$0.030\,64$ (2 s.f.)c$7.996$ (3 s.f.)d$305\,499$ (1 s.f.)

    a $49\,000$   b $0.031$   c $8.00$ (the zeros are significant)   d $300\,000$

  2. Round each number to 1 significant figure to estimate the answer.
    a$612\times38.7$b$\dfrac{48.2\times9.71}{0.52}$c$\dfrac{\sqrt{98.4}+19.6}{2.9}$

    a $600\times40=24\,000$

    b $\dfrac{50\times10}{0.5}=\dfrac{500}{0.5}=1000$

    c $\dfrac{\sqrt{100}+20}{3}=\dfrac{30}{3}=10$

  3. Work out, using the correct order of operations.
    a$\dfrac{5+3\times7-2}{4^2-10}$b$18-(2^3+4)\div3\times2$

    a Top: $5+21-2=24$. Bottom: $16-10=6$. Answer $24\div6=4$.

    b Brackets: $8+4=12$. Then $12\div3\times2=8$ (left to right). $18-8=10$.

  4. Write as a single power.
    a$5^4\times5^{-6}$b$2^{-3}\div2^{-7}$c$(3^{-2})^4$d$\dfrac{7^2\times7^{-5}}{7^{-4}}$

    a $5^{4+(-6)}=5^{-2}$   b $2^{-3-(-7)}=2^{4}$   c $3^{-2\times4}=3^{-8}$

    d Top: $7^{2-5}=7^{-3}$. Then $7^{-3}\div7^{-4}=7^{-3+4}=7^{1}=7$.

  5. Work out the value of each power. Give exact answers.
    a$4^{-2}$b$10^{-3}$c$6^{0}$d$\left(\dfrac12\right)^{-3}$

    a $\dfrac{1}{4^2}=\dfrac{1}{16}$   b $\dfrac{1}{10^3}=0.001$   c $1$ (anything to the power 0 is 1)   d $2^{3}=8$ (flip the fraction, then cube)

  6. Use the prefixes giga (G, $10^9$), mega (M, $10^6$), kilo (k, $10^3$), milli (m, $10^{-3}$), micro (μ, $10^{-6}$) and nano (n, $10^{-9}$).
    • a Write 7.2 GW in watts, in standard form.
    • b Write 450 nm in metres, in standard form.
    • c Write 3 500 000 J in megajoules.
    • d Write 0.006 s in milliseconds.
    • e Write 25 μg in grams, in standard form.

    a $7.2\times10^{9}$ W   b $450\times10^{-9}=4.5\times10^{-7}$ m   c $3\,500\,000\div10^{6}=3.5$ MJ

    d $0.006\times1000=6$ ms   e $25\times10^{-6}=2.5\times10^{-5}$ g

  7. Write a–d in standard form and e–f as ordinary numbers.
    a$64\,000$b$0.000\,302$c$58\times10^{5}$d$0.41\times10^{-3}$e$9.07\times10^{3}$f$2.6\times10^{-5}$

    a $6.4\times10^{4}$   b $3.02\times10^{-4}$   c $5.8\times10^{1}\times10^{5}=5.8\times10^{6}$   d $4.1\times10^{-1}\times10^{-3}=4.1\times10^{-4}$

    e $9070$   f $0.000\,026$

  8. Put these numbers in order, smallest first:   $3.04\times10^{-1}$   $3.1\times10^{2}$   $4.3\times10^{-3}$   $3.4\times10$   $3.4\times10^{-2}$

    Compare the powers of 10 first: $4.3\times10^{-3}$, $3.4\times10^{-2}$, $3.04\times10^{-1}$, $3.4\times10$, $3.1\times10^{2}$ (that is $0.0043,\ 0.034,\ 0.304,\ 34,\ 310$).

  9. Work out without a calculator. Give each answer in standard form.
    a$(3\times10^{4})\times(2\times10^{5})$b$(4\times10^{6})\times(5\times10^{-2})$c$(8.4\times10^{7})\div(2.1\times10^{3})$d$(1.2\times10^{3})\div(4\times10^{-2})$e$6.1\times10^{4}+4.5\times10^{3}$

    a $6\times10^{9}$   b $20\times10^{4}=2\times10^{5}$   c $4\times10^{4}$

    d $0.3\times10^{5}=3\times10^{4}$   e $61\,000+4\,500=65\,500=6.55\times10^{4}$

  10. calc Light travels at $3.0\times10^{8}$ m/s. The Sun is $1.5\times10^{11}$ m from the Earth.
    • a How long does light from the Sun take to reach the Earth? Give your answer in minutes and seconds.
    • b There are about $3.15\times10^{7}$ seconds in a year. How far does light travel in one year? Give your answer in standard form.

    a time $=\dfrac{\text{distance}}{\text{speed}}=\dfrac{1.5\times10^{11}}{3.0\times10^{8}}=5\times10^{2}=500$ s $=8$ min $20$ s.

    b $3.0\times10^{8}\times3.15\times10^{7}=9.45\times10^{15}$ m.

  11. calc A grain of sand has a mass of $6.5\times10^{-7}$ kg. A bucket holds 13 kg of sand.
    • a How many grains of sand are in the bucket? Give your answer in standard form.
    • b A beach contains about $4\times10^{9}$ buckets of sand. Estimate the number of grains on the beach.
    • c A red blood cell is 8 μm wide. How many red blood cells fit side by side across 2 cm?

    a $13\div(6.5\times10^{-7})=2\times10^{7}$ grains.

    b $2\times10^{7}\times4\times10^{9}=8\times10^{16}$ grains.

    c $8$ μm $=8\times10^{-6}$ m and $2$ cm $=2\times10^{-2}$ m, so $\dfrac{2\times10^{-2}}{8\times10^{-6}}=2.5\times10^{3}=2500$ cells.

Check: first significant figure = first non-zero digit · $a^{-n}=\dfrac{1}{a^{n}}$ and $a^{0}=1$ · standard form is $A\times10^{n}$ with $1\le A\lt10$ · multiply the numbers, add the powers.

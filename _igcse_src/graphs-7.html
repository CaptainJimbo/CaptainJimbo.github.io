  2. [6]The graph of $y=2x^2-5x+1$ has been drawn. Find the equation of the straight line that should be drawn to solve each equation.
    a $2x^2-5x-3=0$b $2x^2-7x+1=0$c $2x^2-3x-4=0$

    Add or subtract to turn the left side into $2x^2-5x+1$, and whatever is left on the right is the line.

    a Add 4: $2x^2-5x+1=4$ → line $y=4$.  b Add $2x$: $2x^2-5x+1=2x$ → line $y=2x$.  c Subtract $2x$ and add 5: $2x^2-5x+1=2x+5$ → line $y=2x+5$.

  3. [4]The graph of $y=x^3+2x^2-3x-1$ has been drawn. Find the equation of the line to draw to solve
    a $x^3+2x^2-3x+4=0$b $x^3+2x^2-5x-3=0$

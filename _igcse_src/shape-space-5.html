Shape and Space 5 — Transformations: translation, reflection, rotation, enlargement

Exercises

Squared paper (and tracing paper for rotations) needed. Label every image. To describe a transformation, give all the details: vector / mirror line / angle, direction and centre / scale factor and centre. Marks in brackets. ★ = harder question.

  1. [8]Draw axes from $-6$ to $6$. Draw triangle $J$ with vertices $(-2,3)$, $(2,3)$ and $(1,5)$.
    • a Translate $J$ by the vector $\begin{pmatrix}3\\-3\end{pmatrix}$. Label the image $K$.
    • b Reflect $K$ in the line $y=-x$. Label the image $L$.
    • c Reflect $L$ in the line $x=-3$. Label the image $M$.
    • d Describe fully the single transformation that maps $M$ onto $J$.

    a Add 3 to $x$, subtract 3 from $y$: $K$ is $(1,0)$, $(5,0)$, $(4,2)$.

    b Reflection in $y=-x$ sends $(x,y)\to(-y,-x)$: $L$ is $(0,-1)$, $(0,-5)$, $(-2,-4)$.

    c Reflection in $x=-3$ sends $(x,y)\to(-6-x,\,y)$: $M$ is $(-6,-1)$, $(-6,-5)$, $(-4,-4)$.

    d $M\to J$ is a rotation of $90^\circ$ anticlockwise about $(-6,\,3)$. Check with tracing paper, or: rotating $(-6,-1)$ by $90^\circ$ anticlockwise about $(-6,3)$ gives $(-2,3)$ ✓ and $(-4,-4)$ gives $(1,5)$ ✓.

  2. [12]Draw axes from $-6$ to $6$. Draw triangle $A$ with vertices $(-2,-1)$, $(-1,-1)$ and $(-1,1)$.
    • a Rotate $A$ $90^\circ$ clockwise about $(1,2)$. Label the image $B$.
    • b Enlarge $B$ by scale factor 2, centre $(-4,6)$. Label the image $C$.
    • c Translate $C$ by $\begin{pmatrix}2\\-6\end{pmatrix}$. Label the image $D$.
    • d Rotate $D$ $90^\circ$ anticlockwise about $(-3,-5)$. Label the image $E$.
    • e Describe fully the single transformation that maps $E$ onto $A$.

    a $B$: $(-2,5)$, $(-2,4)$, $(0,4)$.

    b Double each point's distance from $(-4,6)$: $C$: $(0,4)$, $(0,2)$, $(4,2)$.

    c $D$: $(2,-2)$, $(2,-4)$, $(6,-4)$.

    d $E$: $(-6,0)$, $(-4,0)$, $(-4,4)$.

    e $E$ has sides twice as long as $A$ and the same orientation, so it's an enlargement, scale factor $\tfrac{1}{2}$, centre $(2,-2)$. (Lines joining corresponding vertices, e.g. $(-6,0)\to(-2,-1)$ and $(-4,4)\to(-1,1)$, meet at $(2,-2)$.)

  3. [5]The point $P(a,\,b)$ is mapped to $Q(10,\,10)$ by the following transformations in this order: reflection in $y=x$; rotation of $90^\circ$ clockwise about $O$; translation by $\begin{pmatrix}-2\\4\end{pmatrix}$; enlargement of scale factor 2 about $O$. Find $a$ and $b$.

    Track the point: reflection in $y=x$: $(b,a)$. Rotation $90^\circ$ clockwise about $O$ sends $(x,y)\to(y,-x)$: $(a,-b)$. Translation: $(a-2,\,-b+4)$. Enlargement ×2: $(2a-4,\,-2b+8)=(10,10)$. So $2a-4=10 \Rightarrow a=7$ and $-2b+8=10 \Rightarrow b=-1$. $P(7,-1)$.

Total: 25 marks

Describe fully: translation → vector · reflection → mirror line equation · rotation → angle, direction, centre · enlargement → scale factor, centre. Useful rules: $y=x$: $(x,y)\to(y,x)$; $y=-x$: $(x,y)\to(-y,-x)$; $90^\circ$ clockwise about $O$: $(x,y)\to(y,-x)$; $90^\circ$ anticlockwise about $O$: $(x,y)\to(-y,x)$.

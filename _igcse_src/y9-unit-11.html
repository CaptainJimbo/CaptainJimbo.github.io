Exercises

Diagrams are not drawn to scale. Make sure your calculator is in degree mode. ★ = challenge question.

  1. For the angle $\theta$ in this right-angled triangle, write down as fractions
    a$\sin\theta$b$\cos\theta$c$\tan\theta$

    Opposite $=8$, adjacent $=15$, hypotenuse $=17$.   a $\sin\theta=\dfrac{8}{17}$   b $\cos\theta=\dfrac{15}{17}$   c $\tan\theta=\dfrac{8}{15}$

  2. calc Work out the value of $x$ in each triangle, correct to 1 decimal place.

    a Opposite and hypotenuse: $\sin35^\circ=\dfrac{x}{12}$, so $x=12\sin35^\circ=6.9$ cm.

    b Opposite and adjacent: $\tan52^\circ=\dfrac{x}{9}$, so $x=9\tan52^\circ=11.5$ cm.

    c Adjacent and hypotenuse: $\cos28^\circ=\dfrac{x}{20}$, so $x=20\cos28^\circ=17.7$ cm.

  3. calc Work out the value of $x$ in each triangle, correct to 1 decimal place.

    a $\sin40^\circ=\dfrac{7}{x}$, so $x=\dfrac{7}{\sin40^\circ}=10.9$ cm.

    b $\cos63^\circ=\dfrac{5}{x}$, so $x=\dfrac{5}{\cos63^\circ}=11.0$ cm.

    c $\tan25^\circ=\dfrac{6}{x}$, so $x=\dfrac{6}{\tan25^\circ}=12.9$ cm.

  4. calc Work out the size of angle $\theta$ in each triangle, correct to 1 decimal place.

    a $\sin\theta=\dfrac{5}{9}$, so $\theta=\sin^{-1}\left(\dfrac59\right)=33.7^\circ$.

    b $\cos\theta=\dfrac{7}{11}$, so $\theta=\cos^{-1}\left(\dfrac{7}{11}\right)=50.5^\circ$.

    c $\tan\theta=\dfrac{10}{4}$, so $\theta=\tan^{-1}(2.5)=68.2^\circ$.

  5. calc A ladder 4.5 m long leans against a vertical wall. It makes an angle of $68^\circ$ with the horizontal ground.
    • a How far up the wall does the ladder reach?
    • b How far is the foot of the ladder from the wall? Give your answers to 3 significant figures.

    a The ladder is the hypotenuse; the height is opposite $68^\circ$: $4.5\sin68^\circ=4.17$ m.

    b The distance is adjacent to $68^\circ$: $4.5\cos68^\circ=1.69$ m.

  6. calc A wheelchair ramp rises 0.6 m over a horizontal distance of 5 m. Work out the angle the ramp makes with the horizontal, to 1 decimal place.

    Opposite $0.6$, adjacent $5$: $\tan\theta=\dfrac{0.6}{5}=0.12$, so $\theta=\tan^{-1}(0.12)=6.8^\circ$.

  7. calc A ship sails 24 km due north and then 10 km due east.
    • a How far is the ship from its starting point?
    • b Work out the bearing of the ship from its starting point, to the nearest degree.

    a $\sqrt{24^{2}+10^{2}}=\sqrt{676}=26$ km.

    b The angle from north is $\tan^{-1}\left(\dfrac{10}{24}\right)=22.6^\circ$, so the bearing is $023^\circ$.

  8. calc An isosceles triangle has two sides of 10 cm, and its two base angles are each $40^\circ$.
    • a Work out the height of the triangle.
    • b Work out the length of the base.
    • c Work out the area of the triangle.

    a Drop a perpendicular from the top to the base. Height $=10\sin40^\circ=6.43$ cm.

    b Half the base $=10\cos40^\circ=7.66$ cm, so the base is $15.32$ cm.

    c $\tfrac12\times15.32\times6.43=49.2$ cm² (3 s.f.).

  9. calc Kim stands 30 m from the base of a tower on level ground. From her eye level, 1.6 m above the ground, the angle of elevation of the top of the tower is $55^\circ$. Work out the height of the tower, to 1 decimal place.

    Height above eye level $=30\tan55^\circ=42.8$ m. Height of tower $=42.8+1.6=44.4$ m.

  10. calc A circle has centre O and radius 8 cm. AB is a chord and angle $AOB=100^\circ$. Work out the length of AB, to 1 decimal place.

    Triangle AOB is isosceles. The perpendicular from O to AB bisects the angle and the chord, giving a right-angled triangle with angle $50^\circ$ and hypotenuse 8. Half the chord $=8\sin50^\circ=6.13$ cm, so $AB=12.3$ cm.

  11. calc A cuboid has a rectangular base 12 cm by 9 cm and a height of 8 cm.
    • a Work out the length of a diagonal of the base.
    • b Work out the length of the diagonal that joins one bottom corner to the opposite top corner.
    • c Work out the angle this diagonal makes with the base, to 1 decimal place.

    a $\sqrt{12^{2}+9^{2}}=15$ cm.

    b The base diagonal, the height and the space diagonal form a right-angled triangle: $\sqrt{15^{2}+8^{2}}=17$ cm.

    c $\tan\theta=\dfrac{8}{15}$, so $\theta=28.1^\circ$.

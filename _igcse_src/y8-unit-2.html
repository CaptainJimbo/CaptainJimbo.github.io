Pre-IGCSE Mathematics · Year 8 · Unit 2

Equations and formulae — One-step · Two-step · Letters on both sides · Formulae

Exercises

No calculator. Show the balancing step on each line, and check every answer. ★ = challenge question.

A · One-step and two-step equations

  1. Solve these equations.
    a $x-7=12$b $4x=52$c $\dfrac{x}{6}=3$d $x+9=4$

    a Add 7 to both sides: $x=19$.  b Divide both sides by 4: $x=13$.  c Multiply both sides by 6: $x=18$.  d Subtract 9: $x=-5$ (check: $-5+9=4$ ✓).

  2. Solve these equations.
    a $3x-4=20$b $\dfrac{x}{5}+2=6$c $25-4x=5$d $4(x+3)=36$e $\dfrac{2x}{3}=-8$

    a $+4$ then $\div3$: $3x=24$, $x=8$.  b $-2$ then $\times5$: $\dfrac{x}{5}=4$, $x=20$.  c $+4x$ to both sides: $25=5+4x$, so $4x=20$, $x=5$.  d $\div4$ first: $x+3=9$, $x=6$.  e $\times3$: $2x=-24$, $x=-12$.

  3. Solve $\dfrac{2x+7}{3}=5$

    Multiply both sides by 3: $2x+7=15$. Subtract 7: $2x=8$, so $x=4$. Check: $(8+7)\div3=5$ ✓

B · Equations from angles and shapes

  1. Three angles on a straight line are $5x$, $3x$ and $x$. Work out the size of each angle.

    Angles on a straight line add to $180^\circ$: $5x+3x+x=180 \Rightarrow 9x=180 \Rightarrow x=20$. The angles are $100^\circ$, $60^\circ$ and $20^\circ$.

  2. The angles of a triangle are $x$, $2x+15$ and $3x-9$ (in degrees). Work out the size of each angle.

    Angles in a triangle add to $180^\circ$: $x+2x+15+3x-9=180 \Rightarrow 6x+6=180 \Rightarrow x=29$. The angles are $29^\circ$, $73^\circ$ and $78^\circ$ (sum 180 ✓).

  3. Two rectangles have the same area. One is 5 cm by $(x+2)$ cm; the other is 3 cm by $(x+6)$ cm. Work out the value of $x$.

    Equal areas: $5(x+2)=3(x+6) \Rightarrow 5x+10=3x+18 \Rightarrow 2x=8 \Rightarrow x=4$. Check: $5\times6=30$ and $3\times10=30$ ✓

C · Letters on both sides

  1. Solve these equations.
    • a $7m-3=4m+12$
    • b $4(n-1)=2(n+3)$
    • c $3(x+2)=5(x-2)$

    a Subtract $4m$ (the smaller $m$ term): $3m-3=12 \Rightarrow 3m=15 \Rightarrow m=5$.

    b Expand: $4n-4=2n+6 \Rightarrow 2n=10 \Rightarrow n=5$.

    c Expand: $3x+6=5x-10$. Subtract $3x$: $6=2x-10 \Rightarrow 2x=16 \Rightarrow x=8$.

  2. Leo says: "I think of a number, multiply it by 4 and subtract 7. If instead I multiply it by 2 and add 9, I get the same answer." Write an equation and find Leo's number.

    Number $n$: $4n-7=2n+9$. Subtract $2n$: $2n-7=9 \Rightarrow 2n=16 \Rightarrow n=8$. Check: $32-7=25$ and $16+9=25$ ✓

D · Working with formulae

  1. The formula $v=u+at$ gives the speed $v$ of an object after $t$ seconds. Work out $a$ when $u=4$, $t=5$ and $v=29$.

    Substitute: $29=4+5a$. Subtract 4: $5a=25$, so $a=5$.

    • a Write a formula for converting a time in minutes ($m$) into hours ($h$).
    • b Use your formula to convert 390 minutes into hours.

    a There are 60 minutes in an hour, so $h=\dfrac{m}{60}$.  b $h=\dfrac{390}{60}=6.5$ hours.

  3. The perimeter of a rectangle is $P=2(l+w)$.
    • a Work out $P$ when $l=7.5$ cm and $w=4$ cm.
    • b A rectangle has perimeter 30 cm and width 6 cm. Work out its length.

    a $P=2(7.5+4)=2\times11.5=23$ cm.  b $30=2(l+6) \Rightarrow l+6=15 \Rightarrow l=9$ cm.

  4. $3x+2y=24$. If $x$ and $y$ are both positive integers, give all the possible pairs of values of $x$ and $y$.

    $2y$ is even, so $3x$ must be even → $x$ is even. $x=2$: $2y=18$, $y=9$. $x=4$: $2y=12$, $y=6$. $x=6$: $2y=6$, $y=3$. $x=8$ gives $y=0$, not positive. $(2,9)$, $(4,6)$, $(6,3)$.

  5. A square of side $x$ cm is cut from one corner of a rectangle 10 cm by 8 cm. The area of the shape that is left is 71 cm². Work out $x$.

    Area left = rectangle − square: $80-x^2=71 \Rightarrow x^2=9 \Rightarrow x=3$ cm (a length must be positive, so not $-3$).

Check: whatever you do to one side, do to the other · subtract the smaller letter term first · angles on a line 180°, in a triangle 180° · substitute back.

Total: 25 marks

Check: a quadratic graph is a parabola — U if $a>0$, ∩ if $a<0$ — and symmetric · the solutions of $ax^2+bx+c=0$ are where the curve crosses the $x$-axis (two, one or none) · read the greatest/least value at the turning point.

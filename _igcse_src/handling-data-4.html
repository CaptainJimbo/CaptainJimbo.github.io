Handling Data 4 — Experimental and theoretical probability · Sample spaces

Exercises

Give probabilities as fractions in their lowest terms unless asked otherwise. Marks in brackets. ★ = harder question.

  1. [7]Each face of a cube is painted red or blue: two faces red (R), four blue (B). The cube is rolled 12 times:  B B R B B B R B B R B B.
    • a Plot the relative frequency of red after 1, 2, 3, … 12 rolls on a graph.
    • b From the 12 rolls, estimate the probability of (i) a red face, (ii) a blue face.
    • c Comment on your results.

    a Reds so far after each roll: 0,0,1,1,1,1,2,2,2,3,3,3 → relative frequencies $0,0,0.33,0.25,0.2,0.17,0.29,0.25,0.22,0.3,0.27,0.25$. The graph jumps about at first and settles.

    b (i) $\dfrac{3}{12}=\dfrac{1}{4}$  (ii) $\dfrac{9}{12}=\dfrac{3}{4}$.

    c The theoretical probabilities are $\dfrac{2}{6}=\dfrac{1}{3}$ and $\dfrac{2}{3}$. The estimates are close but not equal — 12 rolls is a small sample; with many more rolls the relative frequency should settle near $\dfrac{1}{3}$.

  2. [5]A card is picked at random from an ordinary pack of 52. Find the probability that it is
    a redb a spadec not a queend a black ace.

    a $\dfrac{26}{52}=\dfrac{1}{2}$  b $\dfrac{13}{52}=\dfrac{1}{4}$  c $1-\dfrac{4}{52}=\dfrac{48}{52}=\dfrac{12}{13}$  d $\dfrac{2}{52}=\dfrac{1}{26}$.

  3. [4]A letter is chosen at random from the word ANTIDISESTABLISHMENTARIANISM (28 letters). Find the probability that it is
    a Ab Ic Zd a vowel.

    Count: A×4, I×5, S×4, T×3, N×3, E×2, M×2, D, B, L, H, R (total 28). a $\dfrac{4}{28}=\dfrac{1}{7}$  b $\dfrac{5}{28}$  c $0$ — impossible  d A, I, E: $\dfrac{4+5+2}{28}=\dfrac{11}{28}$.

  4. [9]Two fair dice are thrown and the score is the difference between the two numbers (the larger minus the smaller).
    • a Copy and complete the sample space showing all 36 outcomes.
    −123456

    • b Find the probability that the score is (i) 0, (ii) at least 4, (iii) odd, (iv) a prime number.
    • c The game is played 72 times. How many odd scores would you expect?

    a Each cell is $\lvert\text{row}-\text{column}\rvert$. Frequencies: score 0: 6 (the diagonal), 1: 10, 2: 8, 3: 6, 4: 4, 5: 2 — total 36.

    b (i) $\dfrac{6}{36}=\dfrac{1}{6}$  (ii) scores 4 or 5: $\dfrac{6}{36}=\dfrac{1}{6}$  (iii) 1, 3, 5: $\dfrac{18}{36}=\dfrac{1}{2}$  (iv) 2, 3, 5: $\dfrac{16}{36}=\dfrac{4}{9}$.

    c $\dfrac{1}{2}\times72=36$.

Total: 25 marks

Check: theoretical probability needs equally likely outcomes · $P(\text{not }E)=1-P(E)$ · a sample space lists every outcome once — count carefully · expected frequency = probability × number of trials.

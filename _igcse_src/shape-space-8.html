Shape and Space 8 — Vectors · Column vectors · Vector geometry

Exercises

Calculator allowed. Marks in brackets. ★ = harder question. Bold letters are vectors; $\vert\mathbf{a}\vert$ is the magnitude (length) of $\mathbf{a}$.

  1. [5]$\mathbf{p}=\begin{pmatrix}3\\-1\end{pmatrix}$, $\mathbf{q}=\begin{pmatrix}-2\\4\end{pmatrix}$, $\mathbf{r}=\begin{pmatrix}1\\5\end{pmatrix}$ and $2\mathbf{p}+\mathbf{r}=\mathbf{q}-\mathbf{s}$.
    a Work out $\mathbf{s}$ as a column vector.b Calculate $\vert\mathbf{s}\vert$ to 1 d.p.

    a $\mathbf{s}=\mathbf{q}-2\mathbf{p}-\mathbf{r}=\begin{pmatrix}-2-6-1\\4+2-5\end{pmatrix}=\begin{pmatrix}-9\\1\end{pmatrix}$.  b $\vert\mathbf{s}\vert=\sqrt{(-9)^2+1^2}=\sqrt{82}=9.1$.

  2. [8]Points A and B have position vectors $\overrightarrow{OA}=\begin{pmatrix}4\\7\end{pmatrix}$ and $\overrightarrow{OB}=\begin{pmatrix}16\\12\end{pmatrix}$.
    a Write down $\overrightarrow{AB}$.b Calculate $\vert\overrightarrow{AB}\vert$.c Find the angle $\overrightarrow{AB}$ makes with the $x$-axis, to 1 d.p.d X is the mid-point of AB. Write $\overrightarrow{OX}$ as a column vector.

    a $\overrightarrow{AB}=\overrightarrow{OB}-\overrightarrow{OA}=\begin{pmatrix}12\\5\end{pmatrix}$.  b $\sqrt{12^2+5^2}=\sqrt{169}=13$.  c $\tan\theta=\dfrac{5}{12}\Rightarrow\theta=22.6^\circ$.  d $\overrightarrow{OX}=\overrightarrow{OA}+\tfrac12\overrightarrow{AB}=\begin{pmatrix}4+6\\7+2.5\end{pmatrix}=\begin{pmatrix}10\\9.5\end{pmatrix}$ (the mean of the two position vectors).

  3. [9]OACB is a parallelogram with $\overrightarrow{OA}=2\mathbf{a}$ and $\overrightarrow{OB}=2\mathbf{b}$. M is the mid-point of AC and N is the mid-point of BC.
    Express in terms of $\mathbf{a}$ and $\mathbf{b}$: a $\overrightarrow{OC}$  b $\overrightarrow{OM}$  c $\overrightarrow{ON}$  d $\overrightarrow{NM}$e What do your answers tell you about NM and BA?

    a $\overrightarrow{OC}=\overrightarrow{OA}+\overrightarrow{AC}=2\mathbf{a}+2\mathbf{b}$ (opposite sides of a parallelogram are equal vectors, $\overrightarrow{AC}=\overrightarrow{OB}$).

    b $\overrightarrow{OM}=\overrightarrow{OA}+\tfrac12\overrightarrow{AC}=2\mathbf{a}+\mathbf{b}$.  c $\overrightarrow{ON}=\overrightarrow{OB}+\tfrac12\overrightarrow{BC}=2\mathbf{b}+\mathbf{a}$.

    d $\overrightarrow{NM}=\overrightarrow{OM}-\overrightarrow{ON}=(2\mathbf{a}+\mathbf{b})-(\mathbf{a}+2\mathbf{b})=\mathbf{a}-\mathbf{b}$.

    e $\overrightarrow{BA}=\overrightarrow{OA}-\overrightarrow{OB}=2\mathbf{a}-2\mathbf{b}=2(\mathbf{a}-\mathbf{b})=2\,\overrightarrow{NM}$. So NM is parallel to BA and half its length.

  4. [3]PQRS is a trapezium with PQ parallel to SR. $\overrightarrow{PQ}=4\mathbf{a}+2\mathbf{b}$, $\overrightarrow{QR}=\mathbf{a}-3\mathbf{b}$ and $\overrightarrow{SR}=6\mathbf{a}+k\mathbf{b}$, where $k$ is a constant. Find $k$, showing your working.

    Parallel vectors are multiples of each other: $\overrightarrow{SR}=\lambda\,\overrightarrow{PQ}$, so $6\mathbf{a}+k\mathbf{b}=\lambda(4\mathbf{a}+2\mathbf{b})$. Comparing $\mathbf{a}$ parts: $4\lambda=6\Rightarrow\lambda=1.5$. Comparing $\mathbf{b}$ parts: $k=2\lambda=3$.

Total: 25 marks

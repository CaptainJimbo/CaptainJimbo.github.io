Shape and Space 3 — The tangent ratio · Angles of elevation and depression · Bearings

Exercises

Calculator allowed. Give answers correct to 3 significant figures. Draw a labelled right-angled triangle for every question. Marks in brackets. ★ = harder question.

  1. [3]In a right-angled triangle, the side adjacent to a $38^\circ$ angle is 14 cm long. Find the length of the side opposite the $38^\circ$ angle.

    $\tan38^\circ=\dfrac{\text{opp}}{\text{adj}}=\dfrac{x}{14} \Rightarrow x=14\tan38^\circ=10.9$ cm.

  2. [3]An aeroplane takes off and climbs in a straight line. When it has travelled 600 m horizontally it is 250 m above the ground. Calculate its angle of elevation $\theta$ from the take-off point.

    $\tan\theta=\dfrac{250}{600} \Rightarrow \theta=\tan^{-1}\left(\dfrac{250}{600}\right)=22.6^\circ$.

  3. [4]From the top of a lighthouse, the angle of depression of a small boat is $18^\circ$. The boat is 150 m from the base of the lighthouse. Find the height of the lighthouse.

    The angle of depression from the top equals the angle of elevation from the boat (alternate angles), so in the right-angled triangle: $\tan18^\circ=\dfrac{h}{150} \Rightarrow h=150\tan18^\circ=48.7$ m.

  4. [7]A hot-air balloon rises vertically from a point on the ground. An observer stands 280 m away. When the balloon stops for the first time, its angle of elevation from the observer is $30^\circ$; when it stops at its highest point the angle is $55^\circ$.
    • a Find the height $h_1$ of the first stop and the further height $h_2$ it then rises.
    • b The balloon takes 1 minute to reach the first stop and a further 6 minutes to reach the top. Calculate the average speed of the whole ascent in m/s.

    a $h_1=280\tan30^\circ=161.7$ m. Total height $=280\tan55^\circ=399.9$ m, so $h_2=399.9-161.7=238$ m (3 s.f.).

    b Total ascent $399.9$ m in $7$ min $=420$ s: $\dfrac{399.9}{420}=0.952$ m/s.

  5. [8]A ship sails 8 km due north from a port $P$, then 6 km due east to a point $Q$.
    • a Draw a diagram of the route.
    • b How far is $Q$ from $P$ in a straight line?
    • c Calculate the bearing of $Q$ from $P$.
    • d Write down the bearing of $P$ from $Q$.

    a A right-angled triangle: 8 km up (north), then 6 km to the right (east); $PQ$ is the hypotenuse.

    b Pythagoras: $\sqrt{8^2+6^2}=10$ km.

    c The angle at $P$ between north and $PQ$: $\tan\theta=\dfrac{6}{8} \Rightarrow \theta=36.9^\circ$. Bearing of $Q$ from $P$ is $037^\circ$ (three figures, clockwise from north).

    d The reverse bearing: $36.9+180=216.9\approx217^\circ$.

  6. [4]A vertical mast stands on level ground. From a point 40 m from its base, the angle of elevation of the top is $35^\circ$. From a point 25 m further away, in the same straight line, find the angle of elevation of the top.

    Height of mast: $40\tan35^\circ=28.0$ m. From 65 m away: $\tan\theta=\dfrac{28.0}{65} \Rightarrow \theta=23.3^\circ$ (keep the unrounded height on the calculator).

Total: 29 marks

Check: $\tan\theta=\dfrac{\text{opposite}}{\text{adjacent}}$ — label the sides from the angle you're using · angle of depression = angle of elevation from the other end · bearings: from north, clockwise, three figures · reverse bearing: ± 180°.

Number 5 — Calculators · Estimating · Upper and lower bounds

Exercises

Calculator allowed except in question 4. Marks in brackets. ★ = harder question.

  1. [4]Calculate, giving each answer to 3 s.f.:
    a $2.4+2.4^2+2.4^3$b $\dfrac{2.6\times10^3}{2.6^3}$c $\dfrac{\sqrt{230}}{5.1\times10^3-3.8\times10^2}$d $\dfrac{\sqrt{8.1\times10^3}}{\sqrt{2.5\times10^{-5}}}$

    a $2.4+5.76+13.824=21.984\approx22.0$.  b $2600\div17.576=148$.  c Brackets round the denominator: $15.166\div4720=0.00321=3.21\times10^{-3}$.  d $90\div0.005=18\,000=1.80\times10^4$.

  2. [8]Write the lower and upper bounds of each measurement.
    a 34 m (nearest metre)b 62 kg (nearest kg)c 480 s (nearest 10 s)d 2300 km (nearest 100 km)

    Half a unit either side. a $33.5\le x<34.5$  b $61.5\le x<62.5$  c $475\le x<485$  d $2250\le x<2350$.

  3. [4]$w=\dfrac{x+y}{z}$ where $x=3.5\pm0.5$, $y=2\pm0.5$ and $z=0.8\pm0.1$. Find the lower and upper bounds of $w$.

    Largest $w$: biggest top, smallest bottom: $\dfrac{4+2.5}{0.7}=9.29$. Smallest $w$: $\dfrac{3+1.5}{0.9}=5$. So $5\le w<9.29$ (3 s.f.).

  4. [4]Without a calculator, estimate each value in standard form to 1 s.f. Show your rounding.
    a $2.1\times10^3\times3.9\times10^2$b $\dfrac{6.1\times10^4}{2.9\times10^2}$c $\sqrt{8.9\times10^4}$d $\dfrac{5.1\pi\times10^3}{\sqrt{9.3}}$

    Round everything to 1 s.f. first. a $2\times4\times10^5=8\times10^5$.  b $\dfrac{6}{3}\times10^2=2\times10^2$.  c $\sqrt{9\times10^4}=3\times10^2$.  d $\dfrac{5\times3\times10^3}{3}=5\times10^3$.

  5. [3]A runner covers 400 m, measured to the nearest 10 m, in 52 s, measured to the nearest second. Find the lower and upper bounds of her average speed in m/s.

    Fastest: longest distance over shortest time: $\dfrac{405}{51.5}=7.86$ m/s. Slowest: $\dfrac{395}{52.5}=7.52$ m/s. So $7.52\le v<7.86$ m/s.

  6. [2]The area of a semicircle is measured as 60 m² to the nearest 5 m². Find the lower and upper bounds of its perimeter. (Area of a circle $\pi r^2$, circumference $2\pi r$.)

    Area bounds $57.5\le A<62.5$. For a semicircle $A=\tfrac{1}{2}\pi r^2 \Rightarrow r=\sqrt{\dfrac{2A}{\pi}}$: $r$ from $\sqrt{115/\pi}=6.05$ to $\sqrt{125/\pi}=6.31$ m. Perimeter $=\pi r+2r=r(\pi+2)$: from $31.1$ m to $32.4$ m.

Total: 25 marks

Check: bounds are ± half the unit of rounding, lower bound included, upper not · for a quotient, max = max ÷ min and min = min ÷ max · estimate by rounding to 1 s.f. before calculating · brackets round numerators and denominators on the calculator.

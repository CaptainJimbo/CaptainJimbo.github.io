Pre-IGCSE Mathematics · Year 9 · Unit 8

Scale drawings and measures — Maps and scales · Bearings · Congruence · Similar shapes

Exercises

You need a ruler and a protractor for question 5. ★ = challenge question.

  1. A plan uses a scale of 1 cm to 20 m.
    • a Work out the real length represented by   i 3 cm   ii 7.5 cm   iii 0.4 cm.
    • b Work out the length on the plan that represents   i 90 m   ii 250 m   iii 6 m.

    a $3\times20=60$ m; $7.5\times20=150$ m; $0.4\times20=8$ m.

    b $90\div20=4.5$ cm; $250\div20=12.5$ cm; $6\div20=0.3$ cm.

  2. A map has a scale of $1:50\,000$.
    • a Work out the real distance, in km, represented by 4 cm.
    • b Work out the real distance, in km, represented by 7.2 cm.
    • c What length on the map represents 5 km?
    • d What length on the map represents 800 m?

    a $4\times50\,000=200\,000$ cm $=2$ km.   b $7.2\times50\,000=360\,000$ cm $=3.6$ km.

    c $5$ km $=500\,000$ cm; $500\,000\div50\,000=10$ cm.   d $800$ m $=80\,000$ cm; $80\,000\div50\,000=1.6$ cm.

  3. Write each scale as a ratio in the form $1:n$.
    a1 cm to 5 kmb2 cm to 1 m

    a $5$ km $=500\,000$ cm, so $1:500\,000$.   b $2$ cm to $100$ cm is $1:50$.

  4. Bearings are measured clockwise from north and written with three figures.
    • a Write as a bearing:   due east  ·  south-west  ·  due north.
    • b The bearing of B from A is $065^\circ$. Work out the bearing of A from B.
    • c The bearing of C from D is $310^\circ$. Work out the bearing of D from C.

    a $090^\circ$, $225^\circ$, $000^\circ$.

    b Back bearing: add $180^\circ$: $065+180=245^\circ$.

    c $310-180=130^\circ$.

  5. A ship sails 40 km from a port on a bearing of $050^\circ$, then 30 km on a bearing of $140^\circ$.
    • a Make a scale drawing of the journey, using 1 cm to represent 5 km.
    • b How far is the ship from the port?
    • c On what bearing must the ship sail to return directly to the port?

    a Draw a north line at the port, measure $050^\circ$ and draw a line 8 cm long. Draw a new north line at its end, measure $140^\circ$ and draw a line 6 cm long.

    b The line from the port to the ship measures 10 cm, so 50 km. (The two legs are at right angles, because $140-50=90$, and $\sqrt{40^{2}+30^{2}}=50$.)

    c The ship is on a bearing of about $087^\circ$ from the port, so it must sail on $087+180=267^\circ$.

  6. Two triangles are congruent if they are exactly the same shape and size. Decide whether the two triangles in each part must be congruent. Give the reason (SSS, SAS, ASA or RHS) if they are.
    • a Each has sides 5 cm, 6 cm and 7 cm.
    • b Each has angles $50^\circ$, $60^\circ$ and $70^\circ$.
    • c Each is right-angled with hypotenuse 10 cm and one side 6 cm.
    • d Each has a 7 cm side with angles of $40^\circ$ and $65^\circ$ at its ends.

    a Yes — SSS.

    b No. Equal angles only make the triangles similar; one could be an enlargement of the other.

    c Yes — RHS (right angle, hypotenuse, side).

    d Yes — ASA.

  7. Similar shapes have equal angles and sides in the same ratio.
    • a A triangle has sides 3 cm, 4 cm and 5 cm. A similar triangle has shortest side 7.5 cm. Work out its other two sides.
    • b A rectangle 6 cm by 9 cm is similar to a rectangle 10 cm by $x$ cm, where the 10 cm side matches the 6 cm side. Work out $x$.

    a Scale factor $=7.5\div3=2.5$. Other sides: $4\times2.5=10$ cm and $5\times2.5=12.5$ cm.

    b Scale factor $=10\div6=\tfrac53$, so $x=9\times\tfrac53=15$ cm.

  8. In triangle ABC, D lies on AB and E lies on AC, with DE parallel to BC. $AD=6$ cm, $DB=3$ cm, $AE=8$ cm and $DE=10$ cm.
    • a Explain why triangles ADE and ABC are similar.
    • b Work out the length of BC.
    • c Work out the length of EC.

    a Angle A is in both triangles. Angle $ADE=$ angle $ABC$ and angle $AED=$ angle $ACB$ (corresponding angles, DE parallel to BC). All three angles are equal, so the triangles are similar.

    b Scale factor $=\dfrac{AB}{AD}=\dfrac{9}{6}=1.5$, so $BC=1.5\times10=15$ cm.

    c $AC=1.5\times8=12$ cm, so $EC=12-8=4$ cm.

  9. At the same time of day, a 1.5 m stick casts a shadow 2 m long and a tree casts a shadow 12 m long. Work out the height of the tree.

    The two triangles (object, shadow, sun ray) are similar. Scale factor $=12\div2=6$, so the tree is $1.5\times6=9$ m tall.

  10. ABCD is a parallelogram and AC is one of its diagonals. Prove that triangles ABC and CDA are congruent.

    $AB=CD$ and $BC=DA$ (opposite sides of a parallelogram are equal). $AC$ is common to both triangles. Three pairs of equal sides, so the triangles are congruent (SSS).

  11. A map has a scale of $1:25\,000$. A forest is shown on the map as a rectangle 6 cm by 4 cm. Work out the real area of the forest in km².

    $1$ cm represents $25\,000$ cm $=250$ m $=0.25$ km. Real sides: $6\times0.25=1.5$ km and $4\times0.25=1$ km. Area $=1.5\times1=1.5$ km². (Convert the lengths first — do not scale the 24 cm² directly.)

Check: 1 km = 100 000 cm · bearings: from north, clockwise, three figures · back bearing = bearing ± 180° · similar shapes: find the scale factor from a matching pair of sides first.

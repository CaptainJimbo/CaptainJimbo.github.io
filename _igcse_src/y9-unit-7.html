Pre-IGCSE Mathematics · Year 9 · Unit 7

Multiplicative reasoning — Direct proportion · Translations · Enlargements · Percentage change

Exercises

Questions marked calc may be done with a calculator. Squared paper will help with questions 5–8. ★ = challenge question.

  1. The two quantities in each part are in direct proportion.
    • a 6 notebooks cost €8.40. Work out the cost of 15 notebooks.
    • b A car travels 150 km in 2 hours at a steady speed. How far does it travel in 3.5 hours?
    • c 250 g of flour makes 20 biscuits. How much flour is needed for 36 biscuits? How many biscuits can be made from 1 kg of flour?

    a One notebook costs $8.40\div6=1.40$, so 15 cost $15\times1.40=$ €21.

    b Speed $=150\div2=75$ km/h, so in 3.5 hours it travels $75\times3.5=262.5$ km.

    c $250\div20=12.5$ g per biscuit; $36\times12.5=450$ g. From 1 kg: $1000\div12.5=80$ biscuits.

  2. For each situation, say whether the two quantities are in direct proportion and write a formula.
    • a The cost, $C$ cents, of $x$ pens at 45 cents each.
    • b The cost, $C$ euros, of a taxi journey of $x$ km at €2 per km plus a fixed charge of €3.

    a Yes: $C=45x$ (doubling $x$ doubles $C$, and the graph passes through the origin).

    b No: $C=2x+3$. The fixed €3 means that when $x=0$ the cost is not 0.

  3. $y$ is directly proportional to $x$. When $x=4$, $y=14$.
    • a Write a formula connecting $x$ and $y$.
    • b Work out $y$ when $x=9$.
    • c Work out $x$ when $y=49$.

    a $y=kx$, so $14=4k$ and $k=3.5$: $y=3.5x$.

    b $y=3.5\times9=31.5$   c $49=3.5x$, so $x=14$.

  4. The values of $x$ and $y$ in the table are in direct proportion. Work out $a$ and $b$.
    $x$8$a$20
    $y$2045$b$

    $k=\dfrac{20}{8}=2.5$, so $y=2.5x$. $a=45\div2.5=18$ and $b=2.5\times20=50$.

  5. Triangle A has vertices $(1,1)$, $(3,1)$ and $(1,4)$.
    • a Translate A by the column vector $\begin{pmatrix}4\\-3\end{pmatrix}$. Write down the vertices of the image, B.
    • b Translate B by the column vector $\begin{pmatrix}-6\\5\end{pmatrix}$. Write down the vertices of the image, C.
    • c Write down the column vector of the single translation that maps A onto C.

    a Add 4 to each $x$ and subtract 3 from each $y$: $(5,-2)$, $(7,-2)$, $(5,1)$.

    b $(-1,3)$, $(1,3)$, $(-1,6)$.

    c Add the vectors: $\begin{pmatrix}4-6\\-3+5\end{pmatrix}=\begin{pmatrix}-2\\2\end{pmatrix}$.

  6. Triangle T has vertices $(1,1)$, $(3,1)$ and $(1,2)$. Write down the vertices of the image of T after an enlargement with
    • a scale factor 3, centre $(0,0)$
    • b scale factor 2, centre $(1,1)$.

    a Multiply every coordinate by 3: $(3,3)$, $(9,3)$, $(3,6)$.

    b Double each vertex's distance from $(1,1)$: $(1,1)$ stays, $(3,1)\to(5,1)$, $(1,2)\to(1,3)$.

  7. Triangle P has vertices $(2,2)$, $(4,2)$ and $(2,6)$. Write down the vertices of the image of P after an enlargement with
    • a scale factor $\tfrac12$, centre $(0,0)$
    • b scale factor $-1$, centre $(0,0)$
    • c scale factor $-2$, centre $(1,1)$.

    a Halve every coordinate: $(1,1)$, $(2,1)$, $(1,3)$ — a smaller triangle.

    b $(-2,-2)$, $(-4,-2)$, $(-2,-6)$ — same size, on the opposite side of the centre and upside down.

    c From $(1,1)$ to $(2,2)$ is $\begin{pmatrix}1\\1\end{pmatrix}$; multiply by $-2$ to get $\begin{pmatrix}-2\\-2\end{pmatrix}$, so the image is $(-1,-1)$. Similarly $(4,2)\to(-5,-1)$ and $(2,6)\to(-1,-9)$.

  8. Triangle A has vertices $(1,1)$, $(2,1)$ and $(2,3)$. Triangle B has vertices $(3,3)$, $(6,3)$ and $(6,9)$.
    • a Describe fully the single transformation that maps A onto B.
    • b How many times larger is the area of B than the area of A?

    a Enlargement, scale factor 3, centre $(0,0)$ (every coordinate is multiplied by 3).

    b Area of A $=\tfrac12\times1\times2=1$; area of B $=\tfrac12\times3\times6=9$. The area is multiplied by $3^{2}=9$.

  9. calc Work out the percentage change in each case.
    • a A bike bought for €2400 is sold for €2760.
    • b A car bought for €18 000 is sold for €12 600.
    • c The population of a town rises from 12 500 to 13 250.

    a Profit $360$; $\dfrac{360}{2400}\times100=15\%$ profit.

    b Loss $5400$; $\dfrac{5400}{18\,000}\times100=30\%$ loss.

    c Increase $750$; $\dfrac{750}{12\,500}\times100=6\%$ increase.

  10. A model of a boat is 25 cm long. The real boat is 4.5 m long.
    • a Work out the scale factor of the enlargement from the model to the real boat.
    • b Write the scale of the model as a ratio in the form $1:n$.
    • c The real boat is 1.8 m wide. How wide is the model?

    a $4.5$ m $=450$ cm. Scale factor $=450\div25=18$.

    b $1:18$

    c $1.8$ m $=180$ cm; $180\div18=10$ cm.

  11. A photograph measuring 10 cm by 16 cm is enlarged by scale factor 2.5.
    • a Work out the dimensions of the enlargement.
    • b How many times larger is the area of the enlargement? What do you notice?

    a $25$ cm by $40$ cm.

    b Original area $160$ cm², new area $1000$ cm². $1000\div160=6.25=2.5^{2}$ — the area is multiplied by the square of the scale factor.

Check: direct proportion means $y=kx$ · a negative scale factor puts the image on the opposite side of the centre · percentage change = (change ÷ original) × 100 · lengths × $k$ means areas × $k^{2}$.

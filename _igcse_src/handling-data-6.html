Handling Data 6 — Drawing and interpreting histograms

Exercises

Calculator allowed; graph paper needed. Marks in brackets. ★ = harder question. Frequency density $=\dfrac{\text{frequency}}{\text{class width}}$; area of a bar $=$ frequency.

  1. [10]The masses of 120 apples are recorded in the table.
    mass, $m$ (g)frequency
    $60<m\le80$12
    $80<m\le90$24
    $90<m\le95$20
    $95<m\le100$28
    $100<m\le110$26
    $110<m\le130$10
    a Draw a histogram for this data.b Estimate i the mean mass  ii the median mass  iii how many apples have a mass over 105 g.c State the modal class.

    a Work out each frequency density first (frequency $\div$ width), then draw bars with those heights — unequal widths, no gaps.

    classfrequency $f$widthfrequency densitymid-point $x$$fx$
    $60<m\le80$12200.670840
    $80<m\le90$24102.4852040
    $90<m\le95$205492.51850
    $95<m\le100$2855.697.52730
    $100<m\le110$26102.61052730
    $110<m\le130$10200.51201200

    b i Mean $\approx\dfrac{\sum fx}{\sum f}=\dfrac{11390}{120}=94.9$ g.

    ii The median is the 60th value. Cumulative frequencies: 12, 36, 56, 84 — so it lies in the $95<m\le100$ class, 4 values in. Estimate $95+\dfrac{4}{28}\times5=95.7$ g.

    iii Over 105 g: half the $100<m\le110$ class ($13$) plus all of the last class ($10$): about $23$ apples.

    c The modal class is the one with the highest frequency density: $95<m\le100$ (not the class with the biggest frequency).

  2. [15]The histogram shows the journey times to school of the students in a year group. 8 students took 10 minutes or less.
    a Work out the frequency density of the first class and hence label the vertical axis.b How many students are in the year group?c Estimate the median journey time.
    d Draw a frequency table for the data.e Estimate the mean journey time.f Estimate how many students take longer than the mean time.

    a First class: width 10, frequency 8, so frequency density $=8\div10=0.8$. The first bar reaches 0.8, so the grid lines are $1,2,3,4,5$ and the other bars are at $3.2$, $4.8$, $2.0$ and $0.6$.

    b, d Frequency $=$ density $\times$ width for each bar:

    time, $t$ (min)frequency densitywidthfrequencymid-point$fx$
    $0<t\le10$0.8108540
    $10<t\le15$3.251612.5200
    $15<t\le20$4.852417.5420
    $20<t\le30$2102025500
    $30<t\le50$0.6201240480

    Total $=80$ students.

    c Median $=$ the 40th value. Cumulative: 8, 24, 48 — in the $15<t\le20$ class, 16 values in: $15+\dfrac{16}{24}\times5=18.3$ minutes.

    e Mean $\approx\dfrac{\sum fx}{\sum f}=\dfrac{1640}{80}=20.5$ minutes.

    f Longer than $20.5$ min: the part of the $20<t\le30$ bar above $20.5$ is $\dfrac{30-20.5}{10}\times20=19$, plus the 12 in the last class: about $31$ students.

Total: 25 marks

Check: histogram heights are frequency densities, not frequencies · frequency = density × width (area) · mean: mid-points × frequencies, divide by total · median: find the class of the $\tfrac n2$th value, then go a fraction of the way through it · modal class = tallest bar · unequal widths → no bar may be drawn with its frequency as height.

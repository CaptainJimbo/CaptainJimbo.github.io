Number 4 — Compound percentages · Inverse percentages

Exercises

Calculator allowed. Give money to the nearest cent and other answers to 3 s.f. Marks in brackets. ★ = harder question.

  1. [4]
    • a Increase €1500 by 12%.
    • b Decrease €1500 by 12%.

    a $1500\times1.12=€1680$.  b $1500\times0.88=€1320$.

  2. [3]A painting is bought for €40 000. It gains 8% in value in the first year and a further 5% in the second year. Find its value after the two increases.

    Multiply by both factors: $40\,000\times1.08\times1.05=€45\,360$. (Not $+13\%$: the 5% is taken on the increased value.)

  3. [3]A flat is valued at €800 000. It loses 15% of its value one year and a further 3% the next. Find its value after the two decreases.

    $800\,000\times0.85\times0.97=€659\,600$.

  4. [3]A footballer is valued at €24 million. After a bad season his value depreciates by 20%; after the next, good, season it appreciates by 20%. Find his value after the good season. Is he back to €24 million?

    $24\times0.8\times1.2=€23.04$ million. No — the 20% rise is on a smaller amount than the 20% fall, so he ends 4% below the start ($0.8\times1.2=0.96$).

  5. [4]A diamond is sold for $38 million. If it appreciates at 7% p.a. for the next 5 years, what is its value after 5 years?

    Multiplier $1.07$ applied 5 times: $38\times1.07^5=38\times1.4026=\$53.3$ million (3 s.f.).

  6. [4]A cheetah's top speed is 110 km/h. Suppose it loses 7% of its top speed each year. Find its top speed after 5 years.

    $110\times0.93^5=110\times0.6957=76.5$ km/h (3 s.f.).

  7. [4]
    • a A sun-hat is sold at 25% off for €18. Find its original price.
    • b A drone is sold for €2100, making a profit of 25%. Find the price the seller paid for it.

    a Original $p$: $p\times0.75=18 \Rightarrow p=\dfrac{18}{0.75}=€24$.

    b $p\times1.25=2100 \Rightarrow p=\dfrac{2100}{1.25}=€1680$. (Not $2100\times0.75$ — the 25% is of the cost price, not the selling price.)

  8. [4]A sculpture is valued at €$x$. It appreciates by 2% p.a. for 2 years, then depreciates by 3% p.a. for 3 years, and is then sold for €4 million. Find $x$, to the nearest €1000.

    $x\times1.02^2\times0.97^3=4\,000\,000$. The overall multiplier is $1.0404\times0.912673=0.94954$, so $x=\dfrac{4\,000\,000}{0.94954}=€4\,213\,000$ (nearest €1000).

Total: 29 marks

Check: $n$ years at $R\%$ → multiply by $\left(1\pm\frac{R}{100}\right)^n$ · successive changes multiply · to find an original amount, divide by the multiplier · a 20% fall then a 20% rise is not a return to the start.

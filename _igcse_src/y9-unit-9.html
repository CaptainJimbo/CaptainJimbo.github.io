Pre-IGCSE Mathematics · Year 9 · Unit 9

Accuracy and measures — Speed and rates · Density · Pressure · Upper and lower bounds

Exercises

Questions marked calc may be done with a calculator. ★ = challenge question.

  1. calc Work out the average speed of
    • a a cyclist who rides 18 km in 1 hour 30 minutes
    • b a runner who covers 10 miles in 1 hour 15 minutes.

    a $1$ h $30$ min $=1.5$ h. Speed $=\dfrac{\text{distance}}{\text{time}}=\dfrac{18}{1.5}=12$ km/h.

    b $1$ h $15$ min $=1.25$ h. $\dfrac{10}{1.25}=8$ mph.

  2. calc
    • a A boat travels 140 km at an average speed of 40 km/h. Work out the time taken, in hours and minutes.
    • b A train travels at an average speed of 54 km/h for 2 hours 20 minutes. How far does it travel?

    a Time $=\dfrac{\text{distance}}{\text{speed}}=\dfrac{140}{40}=3.5$ h $=3$ hours $30$ minutes.

    b $2$ h $20$ min $=2\tfrac13$ h. Distance $=$ speed $\times$ time $=54\times2\tfrac13=126$ km.

  3. Convert
    a20 m/s into km/hb90 km/h into m/s.

    a $20\times3600=72\,000$ m per hour $=72$ km/h. (Multiply m/s by 3.6.)

    b $90\,000$ m in $3600$ s: $90\,000\div3600=25$ m/s. (Divide km/h by 3.6.)

  4. A tap fills a 45-litre tank in 6 minutes.
    • a Work out the rate of flow in litres per minute.
    • b How long does the tap take to fill a 180-litre bath?

    a $45\div6=7.5$ litres per minute.

    b $180\div7.5=24$ minutes.

  5. calc Density $=\dfrac{\text{mass}}{\text{volume}}$.
    • a A metal block has volume 240 cm³ and mass 648 g. Work out its density.
    • b Gold has density 19.3 g/cm³. Work out the mass of 50 cm³ of gold.
    • c Ice has density 0.92 g/cm³. Work out the volume of 1.84 kg of ice.

    a $\dfrac{648}{240}=2.7$ g/cm³.

    b Mass $=$ density $\times$ volume $=19.3\times50=965$ g.

    c $1.84$ kg $=1840$ g. Volume $=\dfrac{\text{mass}}{\text{density}}=\dfrac{1840}{0.92}=2000$ cm³.

  6. calc A triangular prism has a right-angled triangle as its cross-section. The two shorter sides of the triangle are 6 cm and 9 cm, and the prism is 20 cm long. Its mass is 4.86 kg. Work out the density of the prism in g/cm³.

    Area of cross-section $=\tfrac12\times6\times9=27$ cm². Volume $=27\times20=540$ cm³. Mass $=4860$ g. Density $=\dfrac{4860}{540}=9$ g/cm³.

  7. calc Pressure $=\dfrac{\text{force}}{\text{area}}$.
    • a A force of 600 N acts on an area of 0.25 m². Work out the pressure.
    • b A pressure of 150 N/cm² acts on an area of 12 cm². Work out the force.
    • c A force of 450 N produces a pressure of 90 N/m². Work out the area.

    a $\dfrac{600}{0.25}=2400$ N/m².

    b Force $=$ pressure $\times$ area $=150\times12=1800$ N.

    c Area $=\dfrac{\text{force}}{\text{pressure}}=\dfrac{450}{90}=5$ m².

  8. Write an inequality to show the lower and upper bounds of each measurement.
    • a a mass $m$ of 64 g, to the nearest gram
    • b a volume $v$ of 350 ml, to the nearest 10 ml
    • c a length $l$ of 7.2 cm, to 1 decimal place
    • d a crowd $n$ of 12 000, to the nearest thousand

    a $63.5\le m\lt64.5$   b $345\le v\lt355$

    c $7.15\le l\lt7.25$   d $11\,500\le n\lt12\,500$

    (Go half a unit of accuracy below and above the rounded value.)

    • a A box holds between 38 and 46 matches. Write this in the form $\square\pm\square$.
    • b A bag of rice is labelled $250$ g $\pm5\%$. Write an inequality for the mass, $m$ grams, of rice in the bag.

    a The middle value is $\dfrac{38+46}{2}=42$ and the values are up to 4 either side: $42\pm4$.

    b $5\%$ of $250=12.5$, so $237.5\le m\le262.5$.

  10. A fence is made from 8 identical panels. Each panel is 1.8 m long, correct to the nearest 0.1 m. Work out the lower bound and the upper bound of the length of the fence.

    Each panel: $1.75\le$ length $\lt1.85$. Lower bound $=8\times1.75=14$ m. Upper bound $=8\times1.85=14.8$ m.

  11. A rectangle measures 12 cm by 7 cm, each correct to the nearest centimetre.
    • a Work out the lower and upper bounds of its area.
    • b Work out the lower and upper bounds of its perimeter.
    • c Tom says “the area is 84 cm², to the nearest cm²”. Is he right? Explain.

    a $11.5\times6.5=74.75$ cm² and $12.5\times7.5=93.75$ cm².

    b $2(11.5+6.5)=36$ cm and $2(12.5+7.5)=40$ cm.

    c No — the area could be anywhere from $74.75$ to $93.75$ cm², a spread of 19 cm², so 84 cm² cannot be trusted to the nearest cm².

  12. A person stands on one stiletto heel, pressing down with a force of 600 N on an area of 1 cm². An elephant stands with a force of 50 000 N on four feet, each of area 1250 cm².
    • a Work out the pressure under the heel and under the elephant's feet.
    • b How many times greater is the pressure under the heel?

    a Heel: $\dfrac{600}{1}=600$ N/cm². Elephant: total area $4\times1250=5000$ cm², so $\dfrac{50\,000}{5000}=10$ N/cm².

    b $600\div10=60$ times greater. The heel's force is much smaller, but it acts on a tiny area.

Check: change minutes into a decimal or fraction of an hour before using a speed formula · m/s × 3.6 = km/h · density = mass ÷ volume · pressure = force ÷ area · bounds are half a unit of accuracy either side.

Graphs 2 — Equation of a line · Sketching · Simultaneous equations graphically

Exercises

Squared paper needed for questions that say draw; a sketch is a rough drawing showing the intercepts and slope. Marks in brackets. ★ = harder question.

A · Finding the equation of a line

  1. [3]Find the equation of the straight line through $A(-2,\,-7)$ and $B(2,\,5)$.

    Gradient $m=\dfrac{5-(-7)}{2-(-2)}=\dfrac{12}{4}=3$. So $y=3x+c$. Substitute $B$: $5=6+c \Rightarrow c=-1$. Equation: $y=3x-1$. Check with $A$: $3(-2)-1=-7$ ✓

  2. [4]A straight line passes through the origin $O$ and the point $A(4,\,-2)$.
    • a Find the equation of $OA$.
    • b Find the equation of the line parallel to $OA$ that passes through $(-3,\,1)$.

    a Through the origin, so $c=0$: $m=\dfrac{-2-0}{4-0}=-\dfrac{1}{2}$, giving $y=-\tfrac{1}{2}x$.

    b Parallel → same gradient: $y=-\tfrac{1}{2}x+c$. Substitute $(-3,1)$: $1=\tfrac{3}{2}+c \Rightarrow c=-\tfrac{1}{2}$. Equation: $y=-\tfrac{1}{2}x-\tfrac{1}{2}$.

B · Sketching

  1. [6]Sketch these graphs, marking where each line crosses the axes.
    a $y=2x-3$b $3x+4y=24$c $y=-x+1$

    a Gradient 2 (steep, rising), $y$-intercept $(0,-3)$; crosses the $x$-axis where $2x-3=0$, at $(1.5,0)$.

    b $x=0 \Rightarrow y=6$; $y=0 \Rightarrow x=8$. Join $(0,6)$ to $(8,0)$: a falling line (gradient $-\tfrac{3}{4}$).

    c Gradient $-1$ (falling at 45°), $y$-intercept $(0,1)$, $x$-intercept $(1,0)$.

C · Simultaneous equations graphically

  1. [6]Solve the simultaneous equations $\;x+y=8\;$ and $\;y=2x-1\;$ graphically. Draw both axes from $-2$ to $8$.

    $x+y=8$ passes through $(0,8)$ and $(8,0)$. $y=2x-1$ passes through $(0,-1)$, $(2,3)$ and $(4,7)$. The lines cross at $(3,5)$, so $x=3$, $y=5$. Check: $3+5=8$ ✓ and $2\times3-1=5$ ✓

  2. [6]At a stationery shop, 3 pens and 2 pencils cost €8, while 1 pen and 4 pencils cost €6. Let a pen cost $x$ euros and a pencil $y$ euros. Write a pair of simultaneous equations and solve them graphically to find the cost of one pen and of one pencil.

    Equations: $3x+2y=8$ and $x+4y=6$.

    First line: $(0,4)$ and $\left(\tfrac{8}{3},0\right)$; second line: $(0,1.5)$ and $(6,0)$. They cross at $(2,1)$: a pen costs €2, a pencil €1. Check: $6+2=8$ ✓, $2+4=6$ ✓

Total: 25 marks

Check: $c$ comes from substituting a point the line passes through · parallel means equal gradients · the solution of simultaneous equations is the crossing point — substitute it back into both equations.

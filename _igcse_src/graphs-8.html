Graphs 8 — Gradient of a curve · Translating, reflecting and stretching graphs

Exercises

Calculator allowed; graph paper for question 2. Marks in brackets. ★ = harder question. Key: blue = $y=\text{f}(x)$, red = first transformed graph, green = second.

  1. [6]The graph of $y=x^3-3x$ is shown. a By drawing tangents, estimate the gradient of the curve at i $x=0$  ii $x=1.5$.  b Where is the gradient zero?

    Draw a straight line touching the curve at the point, then gradient $=\dfrac{\text{rise}}{\text{run}}$ from the tangent.

    a i At $x=0$ the tangent slopes down: gradient $\approx-3$.  ii At $x=1.5$ it slopes steeply up: gradient $\approx3.8$ (anything from about 3.5 to 4 is a reasonable estimate).  b At the turning points, $x=-1$ (maximum) and $x=1$ (minimum).

  2. [5]The temperature $t$ °C of a cup of tea, $m$ minutes after it is poured, is modelled by $t=\dfrac{900}{m+15}$ for $0\le m\le30$. a Copy and complete the table (nearest degree). b Draw the graph of $t$ against $m$. c Find the rate at which the tea is cooling, in °C per minute, when $m=5$.

  3. [4]Here is a sketch of $y=\text{f}(x)$ where $\text{f}(x)=x^3$. a Sketch i $y=\text{f}(x)+2$  ii $y=\text{f}(x-2)$. b Write down where the point $(0,0)$ is mapped to on each graph.

    a $\text{f}(x)+2$ translates the graph up by 2 (red); $\text{f}(x-2)$ translates it right by 2 (green) — the sign inside the bracket works the opposite way to what it looks like.

    b $(0,0)\to(0,2)$ for the first graph and $(0,0)\to(2,0)$ for the second.

  4. [4]The graph of $y=\text{f}(x)$, where $\text{f}(x)=(x-1)^2-1$, has vertex $(1,-1)$ and crosses the $x$-axis at $0$ and $2$. Sketch a $y=2\text{f}(x)$  b $y=\text{f}(2x)$, marking the vertex and intercepts of each.

    a $2\text{f}(x)$: every $y$-coordinate is doubled — a stretch parallel to the $y$-axis, scale factor 2. Vertex $(1,-2)$; $x$-intercepts unchanged at $0$ and $2$ (red).

    b $\text{f}(2x)$: every $x$-coordinate is halved — a stretch parallel to the $x$-axis, scale factor $\tfrac12$. Vertex $(0.5,-1)$; intercepts $0$ and $1$ (green).

  5. [6]The diagram shows $y=\text{f}(x)$ with $\text{f}(x)=(x+1)^2-4$ (vertex $(-1,-4)$, $x$-intercepts $-3$ and $1$). a On the same axes sketch i $y=-\text{f}(x)$  ii $y=\text{f}(-x)$. b Describe the transformation from $y=\text{f}(x)$ to $y=-\text{f}(x)$. c Describe the transformation from $y=\text{f}(x)$ to $y=\text{f}(-x)$.

    a i $-\text{f}(x)$ flips every $y$-value: an upside-down parabola with vertex $(-1,4)$ and the same intercepts $-3$, $1$ (red).  ii $\text{f}(-x)$ flips every $x$-value: vertex $(1,-4)$, intercepts $-1$ and $3$ (green).

    b A reflection in the $x$-axis.  c A reflection in the $y$-axis.

Total: 25 marks

Check: gradient at a point = gradient of the tangent (rise ÷ run, with sign) · $\text{f}(x)+a$ up, $\text{f}(x+a)$ LEFT, $\text{f}(x-a)$ right · $-\text{f}(x)$ reflect in $x$-axis, $\text{f}(-x)$ reflect in $y$-axis · $k\text{f}(x)$ stretch $y$ by $k$; $\text{f}(kx)$ stretch $x$ by $\tfrac1k$ · always say which axis and the scale factor or vector.

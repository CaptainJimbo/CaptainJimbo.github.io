2D shapes and 3D solids — Area · Surface area · Volume · Units

Exercises

Remember the units in every answer: cm² for area, cm³ for volume. A calculator is allowed for questions marked calc. ★ = challenge question.

A · Area of 2D shapes

  1. Work out the area of
    • a a triangle with base 6 cm and perpendicular height 4.5 cm
    • b a parallelogram with base 9 cm and perpendicular height 5 cm
    • c a trapezium with parallel sides 8 cm and 14 cm, and height 5 cm.

    a $\tfrac{1}{2}\times6\times4.5=13.5$ cm².  b $9\times5=45$ cm².  c $\tfrac{1}{2}(8+14)\times5=11\times5=55$ cm².

  2. A shape is made of a rectangle 12 cm wide and 6 cm tall, with a triangle of height 4 cm sitting on its full width. Work out the total area.

    Rectangle $12\times6=72$ cm²; triangle $\tfrac{1}{2}\times12\times4=24$ cm². Total $96$ cm².

  3. Calculate the shaded area:
    • a a square of side 10 cm with a square hole of side 3 cm cut from it
    • b a rectangle 12 cm by 8 cm with a triangle (base 12 cm, height 8 cm) removed.

    a $100-9=91$ cm².  b $96-\tfrac{1}{2}\times12\times8=96-48=48$ cm² — exactly half.

  4. Two triangles have the same area. The first has base 8 cm and height 6 cm. The second has base 12 cm. Work out its height.

    Area $=\tfrac{1}{2}\times8\times6=24$ cm². For the second: $\tfrac{1}{2}\times12\times h=24 \Rightarrow 6h=24 \Rightarrow h=4$ cm.

  5. A parallelogram has base 6 cm and perpendicular height 35 mm. Work out its area in cm².

    Same units first: 35 mm $=3.5$ cm. Area $=6\times3.5=21$ cm².

B · 3D solids

  1. A cuboid measures 3 cm by 4 cm by 7 cm.
    • a Work out its volume.
    • b Work out its surface area.
    • c It stands on its 7 cm by 4 cm face. Give the dimensions of its plan view, front elevation and side elevation.

    a $3\times4\times7=84$ cm³.

    b Three pairs of faces: $2(3\times4)+2(3\times7)+2(4\times7)=24+42+56=122$ cm².

    c Plan (from above): 7 by 4. Front elevation: 7 by 3. Side elevation: 4 by 3.

  2. An L-shaped prism is 8 cm long. Its cross-section is a 10 cm by 2 cm rectangle with a 3 cm by 4 cm rectangle attached. Work out the volume of the prism.

    Area of cross-section $=10\times2+3\times4=32$ cm². Volume $=$ cross-section $\times$ length $=32\times8=256$ cm³.

  3. calc An open box (no lid) is a cuboid 20 cm long, 15 cm wide and 10 cm high. Work out the area of card needed to make it.

    Base $20\times15=300$; two long sides $2\times(20\times10)=400$; two short sides $2\times(15\times10)=300$. Total $1\,000$ cm² (no top).

  4. A cuboid has volume 480 cm³, length 10 cm and height 6 cm. Work out its width.

    $10\times w\times6=480 \Rightarrow 60w=480 \Rightarrow w=8$ cm.

  5. Write down the number of faces, edges and vertices of a a triangular prism, b a square-based pyramid.

    a 5 faces (2 triangles, 3 rectangles), 9 edges, 6 vertices.  b 5 faces (1 square, 4 triangles), 8 edges, 5 vertices.

C · Units and problems

  1. Copy and complete:
    a $2.5\ \text{m}^2=\square\ \text{cm}^2$b $7\,500\ \text{mm}^3=\square\ \text{cm}^3$c $0.8\ \text{litres}=\square\ \text{cm}^3$d $3\ \text{cm}^2=\square\ \text{mm}^2$

    $1\ \text{m}^2=100\times100=10\,000\ \text{cm}^2$; $1\ \text{cm}^3=10\times10\times10=1\,000\ \text{mm}^3$; $1$ litre $=1\,000$ cm³; $1\ \text{cm}^2=100\ \text{mm}^2$.

    a $25\,000$  b $7.5$  c $800$  d $300$.

  2. calc Small boxes measure 10 cm by 5 cm by 4 cm. They are packed into a crate 50 cm by 30 cm by 25 cm, with the 10 cm sides along the 50 cm length, the 5 cm sides along the 30 cm width and the 4 cm sides upright.
    • a How many boxes fit in the crate?
    • b What volume of the crate is left empty?

    a Along the length $50\div10=5$; across $30\div5=6$; layers $25\div4=6$ (remainder 1 cm). $5\times6\times6=180$ boxes.

    b Crate $50\times30\times25=37\,500$ cm³; boxes $180\times200=36\,000$ cm³; empty $1\,500$ cm³ (the 1 cm gap at the top).

  3. A trapezium has parallel sides of 9 cm and 15 cm and an area of 72 cm². Work out its height.

    $\tfrac{1}{2}(9+15)\times h=72 \Rightarrow 12h=72 \Rightarrow h=6$ cm.

Formulas: triangle $\tfrac12 bh$ · parallelogram $bh$ · trapezium $\tfrac12(a+b)h$ · cuboid $lwh$ · prism = cross-section × length · surface area = add every face.

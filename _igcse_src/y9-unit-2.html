Exercises

Questions marked calc need a calculator — use the $\pi$ key. ★ = challenge question.

  1. The diagram shows a triangular prism. Its cross-section is a right-angled triangle.
    • a Work out the volume of the prism.
    • b Work out the surface area of the prism.

    a Area of cross-section $=\tfrac12\times8\times6=24$ cm². Volume $=$ area of cross-section $\times$ length $=24\times15=360$ cm³.

    b Two triangles: $2\times24=48$. Three rectangles: $(6+8+10)\times15=360$. Surface area $=48+360=408$ cm².

  2. A cuboid measures 12 cm by 5 cm by 4 cm.
    • a Work out its surface area.
    • b Work out its volume.
    • c A different prism has a cross-section of area 34 cm² and a volume of 510 cm³. Work out its length.

    a $2\times(12\times5+12\times4+5\times4)=2\times128=256$ cm².

    b $12\times5\times4=240$ cm³.

    c length $=\dfrac{\text{volume}}{\text{area of cross-section}}=\dfrac{510}{34}=15$ cm.

  3. calc For each circle work out i the circumference and ii the area. Give your answers to 2 decimal places.

    b $r=15$ mm. $C=\pi d=\pi\times30=94.25$ mm; $A=\pi\times15^{2}=706.86$ mm².

  4. Give your answers in terms of $\pi$.
    • a Work out the area and the perimeter of a semicircle with diameter 12 cm.
    • b Work out the area and the perimeter of a quarter circle with radius 10 cm.

    a $r=6$. Area $=\tfrac12\pi\times6^{2}=18\pi$ cm². Perimeter $=$ half the circumference $+$ diameter $=6\pi+12$ cm.

    b Area $=\tfrac14\pi\times10^{2}=25\pi$ cm². Perimeter $=\tfrac14\times2\pi\times10+10+10=5\pi+20$ cm.

  5. calc A bicycle wheel has diameter 70 cm.
    • a Work out the circumference of the wheel, to 1 decimal place.
    • b How many complete turns does the wheel make in 1 km?

    a $C=\pi d=\pi\times70=219.9$ cm.

    b $1$ km $=100\,000$ cm. $100\,000\div219.9\ldots=454.7$, so 454 complete turns.

  6. calc A cylinder has radius 4 cm and height 11 cm. Work out, to 2 decimal places,
    aits volumebits total surface area.

    a $V=\pi r^{2}h=\pi\times16\times11=176\pi=552.92$ cm³.

    b $2\pi r^{2}+2\pi rh=32\pi+88\pi=120\pi=376.99$ cm².

  7. calc Each part describes a right-angled triangle. Work out the missing side. Give non-exact answers to 3 significant figures.
    • a shorter sides 9 cm and 12 cm; find the hypotenuse
    • b hypotenuse 13 cm, one side 5 cm
    • c shorter sides 7 cm and 10 cm; find the hypotenuse
    • d hypotenuse 16 cm, one side 9 cm

    a $c^{2}=9^{2}+12^{2}=225$, $c=15$ cm.   b $a^{2}=13^{2}-5^{2}=144$, $a=12$ cm.

    c $c=\sqrt{7^{2}+10^{2}}=\sqrt{149}=12.2$ cm.   d $a=\sqrt{16^{2}-9^{2}}=\sqrt{175}=13.2$ cm.

  8. calc A is the point $(1,2)$ and B is the point $(6,9)$. Work out the length of AB, to 3 significant figures.

    Horizontal change $6-1=5$, vertical change $9-2=7$. $AB=\sqrt{5^{2}+7^{2}}=\sqrt{74}=8.60$ units.

  9. An isosceles triangle has two sides of 10 cm and a base of 12 cm. Work out its area.

    The height splits the base into two 6 cm halves: $h=\sqrt{10^{2}-6^{2}}=\sqrt{64}=8$ cm. Area $=\tfrac12\times12\times8=48$ cm².

  10. calc A cylindrical water tank has diameter 1.2 m and height 2 m. ($1$ m³ $=1000$ litres.)
    • a Work out the capacity of the tank, to the nearest litre.
    • b Water flows in at 15 litres per minute. How long does the empty tank take to fill?

    a $r=0.6$ m. $V=\pi\times0.6^{2}\times2=2.262$ m³ $=2262$ litres.

    b $2262\div15=150.8$ minutes $\approx2$ hours $31$ minutes.

  11. A ladder 6.5 m long leans against a vertical wall with its foot 2.5 m from the wall.
    • a How far up the wall does the ladder reach?
    • b The foot slips to 3.9 m from the wall. How far does the top of the ladder slide down?

    a $h=\sqrt{6.5^{2}-2.5^{2}}=\sqrt{36}=6$ m.

    b New height $=\sqrt{6.5^{2}-3.9^{2}}=\sqrt{27.04}=5.2$ m, so the top slides down $6-5.2=0.8$ m.

Check: volume of a prism = area of cross-section × length · $C=\pi d$, $A=\pi r^{2}$ · cylinder $V=\pi r^{2}h$ · Pythagoras: $c^{2}=a^{2}+b^{2}$ with $c$ the hypotenuse.

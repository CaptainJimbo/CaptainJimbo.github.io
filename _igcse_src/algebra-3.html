Algebra 3 — Factorising · Fractions · Equations with fractions · Simultaneous equations

Exercises

No calculator. Marks in brackets. ★ = harder question. Check every solution in the original equation(s).

  1. [2]Factorise completely:
    a $5x^2+10x$b $18a^2b-12ab^2$

    a HCF $5x$: $5x(x+2)$.  b HCF $6ab$: $6ab(3a-2b)$. Check by expanding.

  2. [4]Simplify:
    a $\dfrac{x^2-3x}{3x}$b $\dfrac{a-ab}{b-b^2}$

    Factorise top and bottom, then cancel the common factor. a $\dfrac{x(x-3)}{3x}=\dfrac{x-3}{3}$.  b $\dfrac{a(1-b)}{b(1-b)}=\dfrac{a}{b}$.

  3. [8]Solve:
    a $\dfrac{2(x-5)}{3}=-4$b $\dfrac{x-2}{4}=\dfrac{x+2}{5}$c $\dfrac{x}{2}-\dfrac{x}{6}=3$d $\dfrac{6}{x}=-\dfrac{2}{5}$

    a Multiply by 3: $2(x-5)=-12 \Rightarrow x-5=-6 \Rightarrow x=-1$.

    b Multiply by 20: $5(x-2)=4(x+2) \Rightarrow 5x-10=4x+8 \Rightarrow x=18$.

    c Multiply by 6: $3x-x=18 \Rightarrow 2x=18 \Rightarrow x=9$.

    d Multiply by $5x$: $30=-2x \Rightarrow x=-15$. Check: $6\div(-15)=-0.4=-\tfrac{2}{5}$ ✓

  4. [6]Solve these simultaneous equations.
    • a $y=2x-5$ and $3x+y=10$
    • b $3x+2y=12$ and $5x-2y=4$

    a Substitution: put $y=2x-5$ into the second: $3x+2x-5=10 \Rightarrow 5x=15 \Rightarrow x=3$, then $y=2(3)-5=1$. Check: $9+1=10$ ✓

    b Elimination: the $y$ terms are $+2y$ and $-2y$, so add: $8x=16 \Rightarrow x=2$. Then $6+2y=12 \Rightarrow y=3$. Check in the second: $10-6=4$ ✓

  5. [5]At a market, 3 oranges and 2 mangoes cost €3.90, while 2 oranges and 5 mangoes cost €5.90. Find the cost of one orange and of one mango.

    Orange $x$, mango $y$: $3x+2y=3.90$ (1), $2x+5y=5.90$ (2). Eliminate $y$: (1)$\times5$: $15x+10y=19.50$; (2)$\times2$: $4x+10y=11.80$. Subtract: $11x=7.70 \Rightarrow x=0.70$. Then $2.10+2y=3.90 \Rightarrow y=0.90$. Orange €0.70, mango €0.90. Check (2): $1.40+4.50=5.90$ ✓

  6. [3]Solve $\ \dfrac{x+1}{2}-\dfrac{2x-1}{3}=1$

    Multiply by 6: $3(x+1)-2(2x-1)=6 \Rightarrow 3x+3-4x+2=6 \Rightarrow -x+5=6 \Rightarrow x=-1$. Check: $0-(-1)=1$ ✓

  7. [4]The sum of two numbers is 27 and their difference is 5. Write two equations and solve them to find the numbers.

    $x+y=27$ and $x-y=5$. Add: $2x=32 \Rightarrow x=16$; then $y=11$. Check: $16-11=5$ ✓

Total: 32 marks

Check: clear fractions by multiplying by the lowest common denominator · elimination — match a coefficient, then add or subtract · substitute the pair back into both equations.

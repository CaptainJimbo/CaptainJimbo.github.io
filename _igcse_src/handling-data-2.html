Handling Data 2 — Frequency tables · Discrete and continuous data

Exercises

Calculator allowed. Squared paper for the frequency polygons. Marks in brackets. ★ = harder question.

  1. [5]Dani records the number of goals ($x$) she scores per game over a season of 20 games.
    $x$0123456
    Frequency1465211
    Last season her mean was 2.1 goals per game. Show that she has improved this season, and write down this season's mode and median.

    $\sum fx=0+4+12+15+8+5+6=50$; $\sum f=20$. Mean $=\dfrac{50}{20}=2.5>2.1$, so she has improved. Mode $=2$ (frequency 6). Median: 20 values → between the 10th and 11th; running totals 1, 5, 11 — both are 2, so median $=2$.

  2. [10]The times ($t$ minutes) taken by 20 students to finish a puzzle were:
    3, 16, 5, 19, 6, 7, 10, 8, 13, 12, 9, 7, 10, 11, 12, 16, 15, 13, 8, 5
    • a Make a frequency table with classes $0\le t<5$, $5\le t<10$, $10\le t<15$, $15\le t\le20$.
    • b Draw a frequency polygon.
    • c Use the table to estimate the mean time, and state the modal class.
    • d Calculate the exact mean from the raw data and explain why it differs from your estimate.

    a Frequencies: $0\le t<5$: 1 · $5\le t<10$: 8 · $10\le t<15$: 7 · $15\le t\le20$: 4 (total 20 ✓).

    b Plot frequency against the class mid-points $2.5,\,7.5,\,12.5,\,17.5$ and join with straight lines.

    c $\sum fx=1(2.5)+8(7.5)+7(12.5)+4(17.5)=2.5+60+87.5+70=220$; estimated mean $=\dfrac{220}{20}=11.0$ min. Modal class $5\le t<10$.

    d Raw total $=205$, exact mean $=10.25$ min. The estimate uses the mid-point of each class instead of the real values — here most values sit below their class mid-points, so the estimate is slightly too high.

  3. [10]A survey of 100 teenagers asked how many hours ($t$) they slept on New Year's Day.
    Hours $t$$0\le t<4$$4\le t<6$$6\le t<8$$8\le t\le12$
    Frequency$p$24$3p$16
    • a Find $p$ and complete the table.
    • b Draw a frequency polygon.
    • c Estimate the mean and state the modal class.
    • d Estimate the percentage of teenagers who slept at least 7 hours.

    a $p+24+3p+16=100 \Rightarrow 4p=60 \Rightarrow p=15$. Frequencies 15, 24, 45, 16.

    b Mid-points 2, 5, 7, 10 against frequency.

    c $\sum fx=15(2)+24(5)+45(7)+16(10)=30+120+315+160=625$; mean $\approx\dfrac{625}{100}=6.25$ h. Modal class $6\le t<8$.

    d Assume the $6\le t<8$ class is spread evenly: half of 45 $=22.5$ slept 7–8 h, plus all 16 of the last class: $38.5\approx39\%$.

  4. [3]The mean of the numbers 4, 7, $x$, 9 and 11 is 8. Find $x$, and then find the median.

    Total $=8\times5=40$, so $x=40-31=9$. In order: 4, 7, 9, 9, 11 → median $9$.

Total: 28 marks

Check: mean from a table $=\dfrac{\sum fx}{\sum f}$ (divide by the total frequency, not the number of rows) · grouped data → mid-points, and the mean is an estimate · modal class = highest frequency · frequency polygon: points at mid-points, joined by straight lines.

Shape and Space 4 — Sine and cosine ratios · Calculating sides and angles

Exercises

Calculator allowed. Give answers correct to 3 significant figures (angles to 1 decimal place where asked). Draw and label a right-angled triangle for each part. Marks in brackets. ★ = harder question.

  1. [4]A window cleaner's ladder is 6 m long and makes an angle of $70^\circ$ with the level ground as it leans against a vertical wall.
    • a Calculate the height of the top of the ladder above the ground.
    • b The cleaner climbs 2.5 m up the ladder. How far is his lower foot from the wall?

    a Height is opposite the $70^\circ$ angle, hypotenuse 6: $h=6\sin70^\circ=5.64$ m.

    b Foot of the ladder from the wall: $6\cos70^\circ=2.052$ m. A point 2.5 m up the ladder is $2.5\cos70^\circ=0.855$ m closer to the wall: $2.052-0.855=1.20$ m.

  2. [8]Three phone masts $A$, $B$ and $C$: $C$ is 5 km due east of $B$; $A$ is due north of $B$ and 8 km from $C$.
    • a Calculate, to 1 decimal place: (i) the angle $\theta$ at $C$, (ii) the bearing of $A$ from $C$, (iii) the bearing of $C$ from $A$.
    • b Calculate the distance $AB$ in km.

    a (i) At $C$ the adjacent side is $BC=5$ and the hypotenuse is $AC=8$: $\cos\theta=\dfrac{5}{8} \Rightarrow \theta=51.3^\circ$.

    (ii) From the north line at $C$, $CA$ lies $90-51.3=38.7^\circ$ anticlockwise (towards the north-west), so the bearing is $360-38.7=321.3^\circ$.

    (iii) Reverse bearing: $321.3-180=141.3^\circ$.

    b $AB$ is opposite $\theta$: $AB=8\sin51.3^\circ=6.24$ km (or Pythagoras: $\sqrt{64-25}=\sqrt{39}=6.24$ ✓).

  3. [13]A circle with centre $O$ represents the Earth (radius $OW=6400$ km). A satellite $S$ is at a distance $OS=42\,000$ km from the centre. $W$ (Washington) and $D$ (Delhi) are points on the circle such that the line $OS$ crosses the chord $WD$ at right angles at $N$, and angle $WOD=100^\circ$.
    • a Calculate, to 3 s.f. or to the nearest degree: (i) $WN$, (ii) $ON$, (iii) $NS$, (iv) angle $ODS$.
    • b Explain briefly why, if angle $ODS$ were less than $90^\circ$, a signal from the satellite could not reach Delhi.
    • c A radio signal is sent from $W$ to $D$ via $S$. Calculate the distance it travels.
    • d Radio signals travel at $300\,000$ km/s. How long does the signal take?

    $ON$ bisects the $100^\circ$ angle (symmetry): angle $WON=50^\circ$, triangle $OWN$ is right-angled at $N$ with hypotenuse 6400.

    a (i) $WN=6400\sin50^\circ=4903$ km $\approx4900$ km. (ii) $ON=6400\cos50^\circ=4114$ km $\approx4110$ km. (iii) $NS=42\,000-4114=37\,886\approx37\,900$ km.

    (iv) In right-angled triangle $DNS$: $\tan(\angle DSN)=\dfrac{4903}{37\,886} \Rightarrow \angle DSN=7.4^\circ$. In triangle $ODS$: $\angle ODS=180-50-7.4=122.6^\circ\approx123^\circ$.

    b At exactly $90^\circ$ the line $DS$ would be a tangent — grazing the Earth. Less than $90^\circ$ means $DS$ would have to pass through the Earth, so $D$ would be over the horizon from $S$.

    c $DS=\sqrt{4903^2+37\,886^2}=38\,200$ km (3 s.f.); by symmetry $WS$ is the same: total $2\times38\,200=76\,400$ km.

    d $\dfrac{76\,400}{300\,000}=0.255$ s — about a quarter of a second.

Total: 25 marks

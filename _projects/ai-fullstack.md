The **backend does the heavy lifting** — APIs, business logic and data — with just enough **frontend** on top to turn it into a working, self-serve **SaaS tool**. Full-stack here means owning the *whole* product end to end, not shipping an API and hoping someone else builds the UI.

- **Backend — the core.** **Python** services (**Django / FastAPI**): the APIs, data models and logic that actually run the product, on **PostgreSQL**.
- **Frontend — enough to ship.** A clean, functional **React** UI — dashboards, forms, the screens users need — kept lightweight and purposeful.
- **And everything in between.** Because full-stack is more than frontend + backend: **auth & permissions**, **hosting & deployment**, **security**, **rate limiting**, **caching**, and **error tracking & logs** — the layers users never see but always feel. That's what separates a demo from a product.
